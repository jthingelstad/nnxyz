# Clean Lines, Flat Color (nifty-faces--line)

Spoken words: 1713, about 11.7 minutes.

## Introduction

Sparer faces on flat color, and the sparer they get, the lighter they feel. The twenty works in this room are drawn with less: a cartoon head in a few strokes, a mask of beads and petals, a diagram of a face in colored line with the ground showing through. Most are from the first four months of 2022, the same months as the skulls and the quiet portraits. This is the artist taking a breath between them.

The line does different things here. On some works it is a cartoon outline, thick and black, with a flat color inside. On others it is a diagram, drawing each part of the face as a shape and leaving the rest to the ground. And on one, the grey face with glasses, it is eight loops and nothing else. Whatever the line does, the ground does the rest, and the grounds are the brightest in the wing: lime, orange, yellow, teal. The color carries the mood, and the mood is a shrug and a grin.

In the earlier rooms the ground is filled or scribbled until the square is full. Here the drawing sits on it like a sticker. Half the canvas is often left bare, and that bareness is the feeling of the room: nothing to hide, nothing to bury, a face put down and left alone. It is the one room in the Faces where the teeth are nearly always a joke.

## Cartoon Heads

December 2021 to February 2022. A scribble mask on white, then a run of cartoon heads: goggle eyes on lime, a black head on red, a face with a peace sign. The line gets thick and the faces get funny.

**#34** (line) An oval mask on white in cyan, pink and green scribble, with two eyes looking up, red lips, and a grin of white teeth, drawn on December 3, 2021, the tangle's method on the bare ground of this room, and the first face here to look pleased about nothing in particular.

**#64** (label) A yellow-green face on lime, hatched in yellow and blue, with two goggle eyes in white boxes under a dark brow and a row of white teeth hanging from the chin. The face is a cartoon: a thick outline, a flat color, two boxes for eyes. The teeth hang below the jaw as a row of white pegs, like the pale head in the painted room, but drawn in line. The cartoon run begins here. The same hanging teeth that made the painted head a saint make this one a goof, and the difference is nothing but the ground: lime instead of a glow.

**#68** (label) A grey mask inside a ring of yellow dots, with two white-dot eyes in dark boxes, a row of pink dots on the brow, and a mouth drawn as a grate of dark bars. Around the yellow dots, rings of grey scallops run to the edges like the rim of a shield. The mask is drawn in soft grey and teal with thin orange line, and it is the most ordered drawing in the room: every mark is a dot, a ring or a bar. The grate of a mouth is the one unfriendly thing in it, and the shield around it says why: this face is armour, and it is calm because nothing gets in.

**#77** (line) A black cartoon head on red, with two white eye boxes and small pupils, a red nose, a wide grin of white teeth, and white marks at the corners like motion lines, a face that has just heard good news.

**#81** (line) A mask head on yellow filled with colored scribble, two white slots for eyes with red slits inside, and a grimace of white teeth in a red mouth, the first face here that is not enjoying itself, and it is still on yellow.

**#82** (label) A black head and shoulders on pink and purple, with a yellow X in one eye, an orange X in the other, small white fangs, and a black hand raised at the right in a peace sign. The figure is one flat black shape outlined in cyan, and the X marks are the only color on it. Seventy-four strokes. It is the one work in the room with a gesture, and the gesture settles the room's question: crossed eyes, fangs, and two fingers up for peace. The threat and the joke are the same figure, and it is waving at you.

## Masks and Emblems

February 19 to March 6, 2022. A skull with a crown for an eye, an owl, a gold blob with star eyes, a skull in profile, a sun of petals and beads, and an angular head in a haze. Emblems, not people: things you could stamp on a flag.

**#89** (line) A cartoon skull in white line under cyan rays, with a yellow crown in a box for one eye and a window of red grid for the other, a grille of white teeth and lime fingers at the side, a skull that has made it to royalty and is pleased to be seen.

**#93** (line) An owl head on orange with pointed ears, two green ringed eyes crossed with red, and a grille of white bars for a mouth, drawn in black, blue and yellow hatching: the box-head mouth on a bird, and the bird looks startled to have it.

**#95** (line) A gold blob of a head curved like a banana on green diagonal stripes, with two red star eyes and a grin of white fangs, the silliest shape in the wing, grinning like it knows.

**#98** (line) A skull in profile on teal, drawn in white and black line, with a sunflower for an eye, a crest of yellow loops, and a grid of thin lines over the whole square, a skull on graph paper, measured and cheerful.

**#102** (label) A sun of a mask on dark green: a round face ringed with yellow petals, each with a dot, and a row of red spikes along the top like a crown. One eye is a white dot in a dark socket, the other a ring with a white center. The mouth is white teeth in a dark oval. Beads of blue, pink and yellow run around the face. With 347 recorded strokes it is the most worked drawing in the room, and the only one with every part in its place. It is a sun that has been up too long: the petals are tidy, the crown is on, and the mouth under them is open in something between a smile and a groan. The room's emblem of effort, drawn as a face that has had enough of being an emblem.

**#105** (line) An angular head on a neck, drawn in black, white and blue line over a haze of soft gold and brown discs, its face a few straight strokes, the quiet portraits' haze with a cartoon drawn on it, and the one head here that is thinking.

## Line Diagrams

March 24 to April 16, 2022. Seven faces drawn as diagrams, from eight grey loops to a machine of rings and teeth on teal. The line stops pretending to be anything but line.

**#113** (label) A face in eight grey loops on grey: two circles for glasses, a curl for a nose, a spiral for a mouth, and a cluster of pink and orange bubbles for hair. Fifty-eight strokes, each one a quick mark, make the sparest drawing in the whole collection. Nothing is filled and nothing is scribbled. It is the sparest face in the Faces, and the most contented. The spiral mouth could be a smile or a hum. After four months of teeth, here is a face with none, and it needs none.

**#114** (line) A head of dark red scribble on grey with two yellow X eyes, a blue bar falling from the face like a tear, and an orange band across the top, the one sad drawing in the room, and the sadness is a single blue stripe.

**#115** (label) A flat face on yellow, striped in blue, green and orange, with two pink X marks for eyes, four small dots across the brow, and a thick white outline with rays. At the lower right a small red face on a stick looks on with its own X eyes. The stripes run down the face and drip at the chin. The outline is drawn with short strokes standing out from it like a child's sun. The big face is solemn, with its mouth shut and its eyes crossed out, and the little face on the stick beside it is grinning. The picture has brought its own heckler.

**#118** (line) A face on navy drawn in blue, pink and yellow line: a ringed eye in a box, a box with an X, a yellow crest, and a long white zigzag of teeth across the lower face, the kit of parts floated on dark, and the zigzag is a laugh.

**#119** (line) A face on olive in white, orange and blue line, with a ringed eye and a spiky eye, two rows of hanging teeth, and a chain of white teeth across the bottom, more teeth than face, and all of them cheerful.

**#120** (none) (title only)

**#123** (label) A face on teal with two eyes like propellers in rings, a red ball for a nose, a bar of white teeth in a red box, and a crown of yellow and blue ovals. Everything is drawn in line, and every part is a separate shape: a ring, a box, a ball, a bar. Three weeks after the face in eight grey loops, this is the diagram with all its parts filled in. The last work in the room, from April 16, 2022. The propeller eyes spin and the red nose is a clown's, and the whole machine is built to be looked at and liked. The room ends on the kit assembled as a toy, which is where the Faces began.

## Closing

Look again at the face in eight grey loops, and then at the face of propellers and teeth on teal. Both are line on a flat ground. One leaves almost everything out and is at peace with it. The other draws every part and still leaves the ground showing. That is the range of this room: from a face that needs nothing to a face that has everything and is still not sure it is a face.
