# Faces in the Tangle (nifty-faces--tangle)

Spoken words: 1630, about 10.9 minutes.

## Introduction

Faces found in a tangle, and most of them are people. The works in this room are drawn as dense, many-colored scribble, and the face comes out of it: a person with glasses, a man in a hat, a girl in a cap, a screaming head in a halo of white. Twenty-one works, from the second drawing in the series on October 27, 2021, to April 2022. Where the box heads next door are toys, these are likenesses, and the feeling in them is the feeling of recognizing someone.

The scribble is not fill. It is the drawing. Hair, cheeks, beards and backgrounds are all the same looping line in different colors, and the features are the places where the line gets dense or changes color. Glasses are two loops. A mouth is a red knot with white inside it. The method is generous: it lets a face arrive slowly, out of noise, the way a face arrives in a crowd. That is why the first works here feel warm even when they grin with fangs.

Watch how the tangle thins, and how the mood goes with it. In the first weeks the whole square is scribble and the faces are amused, dazed or shouting with pleasure. In February two faces are painted with the tangle only on top, and they are angry. By March the scribble has become hatching, and the faces snarl. The line that found friends in the autumn is, by spring, finding people who have had enough.

## Likenesses in Scribble

Five works from October 27 to November 27, 2021. People with glasses and hats, a face lost in magenta, and the first scream, which is a shout of joy.

**#2** (label) A face with rectangular glasses and a fanged grin, drawn entirely in scribble: yellow and red for the hair, green and blue for the cheeks, a red knot for the mouth with white teeth inside it. The glasses are two white loops joined by a bar. The second work in the series, from October 27, 2021. Where the first was a box, this is a person, and the way the line finds a likeness is the method of the whole room. The fangs should be frightening and are not. This is someone laughing too hard at their own joke, and the room starts in good company.

**#4** (line) A face with round purple glasses and a small red mouth, its hair yellow and blue scribble, drawn in loops of every color on a pale ground, a face caught mid-sentence.

**#5** (label) A face in a storm of magenta line. Twenty-eight strokes make the whole drawing, but each stroke is long, looping across the square again and again, and the face is found where the line turns blue and green: two eyes, a nose, a closed mouth. The face does not stand out from the tangle. It is the tangle, looked at long enough. The mouth is closed and the eyes are level, and of all the faces in the Faces this is the calmest. It is a picture of someone thinking, drawn by not deciding where the thinking stops.

**#7** (line) A head on red with two square goggle eyes, a big grin of pointed white teeth, two ears, and orange spiky hair, drawn in green and blue scribble, delighted and slightly deranged.

**#8** (label) A screaming head in a halo of white bands. The face is green and teal scribble with two dark eyes, a nose and an open mouth with white teeth, and around it concentric bands of white and green run to the edges like ripples. The bands are the only calm thing in the picture. The series' first scream, from November 26, 2021, and it is not a scream of pain: the eyes are half shut and the bands around the head are the sound going out into the room. This is someone yelling with happiness, and the whole square rings with it.

## Hair, Hats and Grins

November 27 to December 6, 2021. Eight heads in ten days: a man in a hat, a girl in a cap, white fur, blond scribble, confetti. The tangle is at its thickest and its friendliest.

**#9** (line) A grinning face in purple, orange and yellow scribble, with a brow of dense black loops and a small open mouth, sly and pleased.

**#11** (line) A man in a wide-brimmed hat, drawn in dark purple and yellow scribble so dense the face is almost solid, on a pale ground, the one face in the room that looks tired already.

**#15** (line) A spiky dark head on pink with X marks for eyes and a frown, drawn in green, brown and red scribble, the first cross face in the room, sulking on the brightest ground.

**#20** (label) A girl in a yellow cap and round glasses, inside a frame of pink, yellow and cyan lines, her hair falling in pink and black scribble. The cap is drawn as a box of yellow loops with a brim. Her mouth is a small red mark. It is the only framed work in the room and the only one where the scribble is held inside an edge. She is also the only face here that is not performing. She is just under her cap, looking out, and the frame around her is the closest the series comes to tenderness.

**#22** (none) (title only)

**#25** (label) A grinning face covered in white fur, the hair and beard drawn in short white and lilac strokes standing out in every direction, with a wide grin of white teeth and small eyes. The ground is orange and yellow loops. The fur is the scribble turned into hair, and the grin is the widest in the room. It is a grin with nothing behind it but more fur, and that is the joke: the whole creature is hair and teeth, and it could not be happier about it.

**#30** (line) A head of blond scribble with two dark eyes and a mouth of white block teeth in a red box, on a ground of colored bands, the box-head mouth dropped into a tangle head, and the result is startled.

**#33** (line) A head on maroon with big ears, two target eyes, and red lips drawn in white and yellow line over red scribble, all ears and all eyes, listening hard.

**#41** (label) A face in confetti. Small dabs of orange, blue, pink and green cover the whole square, and a small face sits in the middle of them with two dark eyes and a red mouth. The dabs are not loops; each one is a short stroke, and there are more than a thousand of them. Its 1,020 recorded strokes are the most of any work in the Faces. All that confetti, and the face in the middle of it is small and serious, with its mouth shut. The party is everywhere except on the face, and that is the picture.

## Screams and Snarls

December 6, 2021 to April 2022. Seven heads, spaced weeks apart. The scribble turns to hatching, the mouths open, and the faces stop being glad to see you.

**#47** (line) A screaming egg of a head on brown, drawn in blue and cyan scribble, its mouth a wide open oval with white teeth, ten days after the scream in the halo, and this time there is no halo and no joy in it.

**#86** (label) A face on purple with long lashes, angry brows and a snarl, painted in yellow and brown with scribble over it. The eyes are white with green irises under thick black lashes. The mouth is open on a row of white teeth, the lip pulled up. Below the face the body is a dark mass of black and yellow hatching. The face is painted first and the scribble is drawn on top, a change from the earlier rooms where the scribble was the face. It is also the first face in the room that is simply angry, with no grin hidden in it. The brows do it. Everything the autumn scribble found by accident, this face means.

**#87** (line) A face on yellow with brown hair, blue eyes under dark lashes, and a snarl of white teeth, painted an hour after the purple one, and just as angry, with the light turned up.

**#104** (line) A profile on dark blue with closed eyes, the face in white and red scribble, the hair a mass of red and black loops, the one head here turned away, as if the room had become too loud.

**#110** (line) A hatched head on orange with yellow X marks for eyes and a thin grin of white teeth, drawn in brown, green and black hatching, the grin gone thin and the eyes gone.

**#111** (label) A tall screaming face on pink and purple, its mouth open on two rows of white fangs, its eyes red rings, with a second face behind it at the right. The line is black and white with yellow and blue over it. A month after the snarl on purple, the mouth has opened all the way and the face has doubled. The second face is the first face's shadow, and it is screaming too. Set this beside the shout of joy in the white halo from November and the room's whole arc is there: the same open mouth, four months apart, and nothing left to be glad about.

**#127** (line) A bearded face on orange with square glasses and a grimace of white teeth, the beard drawn in green and white hatching: the man in glasses from the first day of the room, back in April, and no longer laughing.

## Closing

In the magenta storm the scribble is everything and the face is barely there, and it is at peace. On the purple snarl the face is painted and the scribble only sits on top, and it is furious. The line did the same work in both. What changed between November and February is what the line found when it looked.
