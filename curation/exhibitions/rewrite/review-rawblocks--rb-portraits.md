# Portraits (rawblocks--rb-portraits)

Spoken words: 1917, about 12.8 minutes.

## Introduction

People, painted fast. The Rawblocks series opens with a run of portraits, most of them drawn between November 5 and 9, 2022: a man in a plaid shirt with a cigarette, a hunched man in a cap, a figure in a hooded cloak of polka dots, a man in a beanie and beard. They are painted in soft discs of color with a loose line drawn over them, and the line is what makes them people. They are the most human pictures nnxyz made, and they were made in the week the crypto world's largest exchange collapsed.

Each one wears something. A cap, a bandana, sunglasses, a fur coat, a hoodie. The clothes are often more worked than the face, and the face is a few strokes: two eyes, a red mouth, a cigarette. The grounds are flat and plain, grey, olive, blue, so the figure is all there is. Nobody here is grinning. After a year of masks, the artist sat down on a Saturday evening in November and painted six people in two hours, and they look like people who have been up too late and heard bad news.

FTX began to fail on November 6, 2022, and filed for bankruptcy on the 11th. The artist posted once that week, on the 10th, that it was always funny to read that marketplaces think they have a choice and artists think they don't. The room opens with three cartoon heads from the first night and closes with two painted figures from January 2023, drawn after two months away. Watch how the painting changes between them, from a round yellow head to a woman in yellow light with no face at all.

## The First Night

October 30 and 31, 2022. Three cartoon heads to begin: a round yellow head in pink glasses, a face in sunglasses in orange rays, and a red-haired head on legs. Two weeks after handing the whole of New Nifty on the Block to the collector, the artist started again.

**#3** (label) A round yellow head wearing pink glasses, with a small red mouth and one curl of hair, on a body in a blue coat holding out a black glove. The ground is teal with soft discs, and a row of yellow triangles stands on the head like a crown of spikes. It is the first Rawblocks work after the two masks of May, drawn October 30, 2022, and it is a cartoon: a circle for a head, a dot for a nose. It is also a sun with a face, the figure that ended New Nifty on the Block two weeks earlier, now wearing glasses and offering a hand. The new series begins where the old one stopped, and in a better mood.

**#6** (line) A face in dark sunglasses drawn in yellow and purple line over a ground of orange rays, its mouth open on a row of white teeth.

**#7** (line) A red-haired head on two black legs walks across orange, grinning with white fangs, one arm out, its hair a cloud of red scribble.

## One Evening in November

November 5, 2022. Six portraits in two hours, from a man in plaid to a hunched man in a cap. The next day the run on FTX began.

**#8** (label) A man with a red cap of hair and blue eyes stands in a plaid shirt, painted in grey, brown and teal with white line over it, a cigarette at his lips. The ground is brown with dark shapes at the right. The shirt is drawn as a grid of lines over dark paint, and the face is a few strokes: two blue eyes, a red mouth, a line for the nose. The November portraits begin here, on the evening of Saturday, November 5, 2022. He is not a mask and not a cartoon. He is a man smoking, looking slightly to one side, drawn by someone who had spent a year not drawing people. The day after, the exchange began to fail, and the artist kept painting faces like this one through the week.

**#9** (line) A man in a blue striped shirt sits with his hands on his knees, his trousers black with white stripes, on a grey ground, his small face painted in brown, waiting for something.

**#10** (line) A man in a camouflage shirt raises one arm against dark green, his hair blue, his mouth red, drawn in white line over painted tan and brown.

**#11** (label) A figure in a hooded cloak of blue and white stripes with yellow polka dots, a brown face inside the hood drawn in orange scribble with one blue eye, on teal inside a loose white frame. The cloak is painted in soft blue with the stripes and dots drawn over it. The face is small and the cloak is huge, and the frame around the whole thing is drawn in a few white strokes, like a window. Someone wrapped up against weather, seen through a window: it is the tenderest of the November figures, and the one most clearly drawn from feeling, with the formula set aside. The hood is the year's mask, turned soft.

**#12** (line) A hunched man in a dark cap looks down against purple, his face painted in gold and brown with a red eye, a band of pink light crossing behind him, the last of the six that evening, and the saddest.

**#13** (none) (title only)

## Glasses, Hoodies and Hats

November 6 to 8, 2022. Eight portraits: glasses and curly hair, pink sunglasses, a cap and a cigarette, a bandana, a beanie and a beard. These are the days the exchange was failing, and the faces are the calmest the artist ever painted.

**#14** (line) A man with curly hair and glasses in profile on olive, painted in grey and lilac, with red lips and a dark coat, the glasses drawn as a single lens, on the day the run on the exchange began.

**#15** (line) A face drawn in black line with curly hair, lit by rainbow rays from behind, its eyes yellow and its mouth a grin of teeth.

**#16** (label) A man in pink sunglasses and a blue hood, painted in brown and grey, his hair a crest of red and orange strokes, his mouth open on orange teeth with red drips falling from it. The sunglasses are one pink shape across the face with a dark lens. The hood is blue with white line. With 585 recorded strokes it is one of the most worked drawings in the room, and the drips are the one violent mark in a room of calm faces. Drawn on November 6, 2022. The man is dressed for a party and something is coming out of his mouth, and the sunglasses mean nobody can see what he thinks about it.

**#17** (label) A man in a blue cap and sunglasses with a red beard smokes a cigarette, in profile on olive with a field of white dots behind him. The cap is drawn in blue with a dark brim, the sunglasses are black with a purple lens, and the beard is orange scribble. His jacket is red with white sleeves. The cigarette is drawn in white with a red tip. It is the room's most complete figure: hat, glasses, beard, jacket, cigarette, all in one. He is also the most composed. On a day when the people who held money on the exchange were trying to get it out, the artist painted a man taking a drag with his eyes hidden and his beard neat, and gave him a field of polka dots to stand in.

**#18** (label) A profile in a red bandana and glasses, with a gold earring drawn as a spiral, red lips, and a yellow jacket, on a ground of soft yellow and pink discs. The bandana is drawn in red scribble with a blue band across it. The face is painted in pink and lilac and drawn over in black. At 708 recorded strokes, no other work in the room took as much drawing. The earring is the spiral that was an eye all year, moved to the ear, and the red lips are shut. This is the one portrait in the room that looks like it was made from affection for the person in it, whoever they were.

**#21** (line) A painted head with green curly hair and a small red mouth on blue, its face grey and purple with two round eyes, a hand at the shoulder, drawn on November 8, the day the exchange stopped paying out.

**#22** (line) The same head, half an hour later, with red goggles and a beam of gold pouring from its mouth to the right.

**#23** (label) A man in a red beanie with a brown beard and blue eyes, in a yellow jacket, on blue. The face is painted in dark blue and brown with colored scribble over it, and the eyes are two rings under drawn lashes. The beanie is red with a folded brim. The jacket is yellow with a row of buttons. It is the last of the November portraits, drawn November 8, with 592 recorded strokes. The eyes are ringed like the masks' eyes but they have lashes, and they are looking straight out, worried. He is the one man in the room who meets your eye, and he was drawn the night the exchange halted withdrawals.

## Cartoons and Two Painted Figures

November 9, 2022, three cartoon heads with horns and goggles. Then, after ten weeks, two painted figures from January 23, 2023, from the week the artist came back to the board.

**#26** (line) A cartoon head on red with pink lashes, yellow eyes, pink cheeks, a mustache and a horn, two black hands raised at the sides.

**#27** (line) A dark cartoon head on purple with a crown of red dots and white spikes, orange lashes, and a beam of orange light crossing from the left, on a thin neck of bones.

**#28** (line) A head with curly yellow hair, blue goggles, a red clown nose and white lips, on red, with green dots on the cheeks and a striped wand at the right, the clown drawn on the day the deal to save the exchange fell through.

**#51** (label) A woman with dark hair in yellow light, her face a cloud of pale discs with no features but a blue mark and a small red mouth, her hands drawn in black at the lower corners. Sixty-eight strokes. Ten weeks after the man in plaid, the painting has gone soft: the light is the subject, and the face is almost gone. It was drawn on January 23, 2023, in the week the artist returned to the board after two months of silence. The collector had bought the dark head drawn five days earlier to celebrate the return. This is what the return looked like when it was quiet: a person in light, barely there, and the hands at the corners the only solid things.

**#52** (line) A figure with purple hair looks up on black, its face painted in gold and green with scribble over it, one arm raised and the other reaching out, drawn in brown line, the last person in the series.

## Closing

The man in plaid with his cigarette was drawn in a few strokes on the first evening of November, with the exchange that held half the market's money three days from going under. The woman in yellow light came in January, when the artist came back. Both are people. One is drawn in line over paint. The other is paint with almost no line, and the face has dissolved into the light. Between them the masks of the year were set down, and this room is what the artist drew without them.
