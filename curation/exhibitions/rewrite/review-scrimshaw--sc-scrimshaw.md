# Scrimshaw (scrimshaw--sc-scrimshaw)

Spoken words: 1963, about 13.1 minutes.

## Introduction

Heads by Scrimshaw, a different artist. These twenty-five works are the earliest in the collection, most of them drawn on February 16, 2021, the rest over the following days, with a few returns as late as December 2023. They hang together because they share one way of drawing: a head built from loose, looping colored line, on a bright ground. They were on the board eight months before nnxyz's first ink, and they bare their teeth the same way.

The line is the whole method. A head is a loop, then another loop in another color, then a third, until the face is a weave of blue, red and yellow lines that never quite close. Eyes are rings. Teeth are zigzag. Hair is a crest of straight strokes. Who saw whom is not in the record, and the gallery does not guess. What the record holds is a thread: nnxyz liked three of Scrimshaw's inks on the board, and in April 2022 Scrimshaw received the sixth of the Abstract paintings as a gift. Two artists who drew a mouth the same way, and one of them said thank you in paint.

The collector is the other thread. Jamie Thingelstad bought the greeting GM in April 2022, three weeks before his first nnxyz purchase, and then, on two evenings in August 2022, bought twenty-one of these heads within ten minutes each night: the first night was the one he bought the first two nnxyz drawings, and the second was the day before he bought the Faces origin piece. He has said he sees a related visual character in Scrimshaw. The wall agrees. Watch the halos: several heads have a ring or an oval drawn above them, and it is one of the marks that sets these apart from the nnxyz masks in the other wings.

## One Evening in February

February 16, 2021, from the afternoon to midnight. Ten drawings in seven hours, from a pale face in blue line to a long-haired face in pink. The collector bought all ten on the evening of August 11, 2022, Minneapolis time, within five minutes.

**Scrimshaw1** (label) A face drawn in one blue line over a pale ground of yellow and lilac discs, with a square chin, two small eyes and a zigzag of teeth, a crest of blue strokes above. The line wanders from the brow down the nose to the jaw and back, and the pale color behind it is laid in soft overlapping shapes. It is the earliest work in the whole collection, from the afternoon of February 16, 2021, when Nifty Ink was six months old. The face is calm and the teeth are a zigzag. Before nnxyz drew a single ink, this mouth was on the board, drawn by someone else, and the collector bought it eighteen months later on the night he began buying nnxyz in earnest.

**Scrimshaw2** (line) A skull drawn in looping blue, white and yellow line on blue, with ringed eyes and a mouth of purple teeth, vertical strokes falling around it like hair.

**Scrimshaw3** (label) Three heads outlined in thick black line on purple, side by side, their faces filled with orange, white and blue scribble, their noses and mouths drawn in a few black strokes. The black outline runs around all three without a break. It is the first drawing in the collection with more than one face, and the one where the line is heaviest. Three heads in one outline is a family or a band, and the grins are mild. Ten months later nnxyz drew a crowd of heads for the first New Nifty on the Block, in a different hand, with more teeth.

**Scrimshaw4** (line) A face in dark purple line on light blue, with ringed eyes, a red mouth of teeth, and a crest of purple strokes, drawn in sixty-nine strokes.

**Scrimshaw5** (label) A skull face on yellow framed in red line, with a cross on its brow, two ringed eyes in red and blue, a mouth of white zigzag teeth, and green hair drawn in straight strokes. Blue zigzag runs across the lower half. The red frame is a box drawn around the head in a few strokes. The face is a skull, but with hair and a frame it is also a portrait. The ringed eyes, the zigzag teeth, the red frame: three of the parts nnxyz would build a year of masks from are on this one yellow square from February 2021. The gallery states the date and stops there.

**Trio1** (label) Three narrow figures stand together in a field of orange, their upright bodies assembled from red, blue, white and yellow lines. Looking can alternate between three characters and one interwoven pattern. They have halos, or hats, drawn as loops above the heads, and they are the first standing figures in the collection.

**Scrimshaw6** (line) A painted face in brown on yellow with long hair, two blue-ringed eyes and a small mouth, blue line drawn over the paint.

**Scrimshaw6 (green)** (label) A large head nearly fills the square, assembled from thick red, black and yellow lines. Its eyes receive different frames: a purple ring on one side and orange loops on the other. Yellow and blue ovals organize the dark mouth into repeated rows. Two eyes that do not match and a mouth of ovals in rows: of all the Scrimshaw heads this is the one that most resembles the nnxyz masks, and it was drawn in February 2021. The title repeats a number, and the collection keeps both.

**Scrimshaw7** (line) A pale face with a spiral drawn above it, rays of red and purple fanning from the spiral, two ringed eyes and a mouth of white zigzag teeth.

**Scrimshaw8** (line) A face with long black hair drawn in straight strokes over pink and teal lines, on yellow, a pink halo above, the face a few black strokes.

## The Next Days

February 17 to 22, 2021. A faint face on orange, a haloed face on purple, a grid of sixteen faces, and three more heads.

**Scrimshaw9** (line) A faint face in white, blue and yellow line on orange, tipped to the right, with blue strokes for hair, in fifty-two strokes.

**Scrimshaw8** (line) A face with long black hair drawn in straight strokes over pink and teal lines, on yellow, a pink halo above, the face a few black strokes.

**Scrimshaw12** (label) Sixteen faces in a grid of four by four on purple, each drawn in loose black and white line with a different expression: one grins, one frowns, one has a mouth of teeth, one looks sideways. The grid lines are drawn by hand. It is the most densely drawn work in the Scrimshaw collection, and the only grid. Sixteen moods on one page, drawn in a sitting on February 18, 2021: it is the room's clearest statement that the face was the subject and the expression was the game, and the same game nnxyz would play across three hundred masks.

**Scrimshaw13** (line) A face with a wide black ring above it like a hat, drawn in black, red and blue line on pale green, with a target eye and an X eye, a red mouth and a spiral at the chin, the X and the target in one face in February 2021.

**Scrimshaw14** (line) A blue head on yellow with a crest of blue strokes, two ringed eyes, and a mouth open on black zigzag teeth, a spiral at the upper right.

**Scrimshaw15** (line) A long-haired face on blue with red and purple hair falling in long strokes, two ringed eyes with crosses, and a mouth of black zigzag teeth.

## Figures and Later Heads

March to August 2021. Five figures standing together, a boxed face, a spiky head, a crossed eye, and two heads on black.

**Stick Together** (label) Five upright figures stand close together, their long colored bodies filling the square. Thin lines cross between them, while differences in facial marks and color keep each figure distinct. Loops above their heads repeat the same gesture across the group. The title is an instruction, from March 2021, a year before the market fell, and it is the gentlest picture in the wing: five people in a row, each a different color, holding still together. The collector bought it on August 12, 2022, the day after nnxyz began burning the unsold inks.

**Scrimshaw 18** (line) A boxed face on orange drawn in purple, blue and yellow line, two ringed eyes, a mouth of zigzag, and a spiral at the chin, with loops running out past the box.

**Scrimshaw 21** (line) A spiky-haired head on yellow, drawn in black, red and blue line, with a ringed eye, a long nose and a mouth of white teeth, in sixty-nine strokes.

**Scrimshaw 24** (label) A face on purple with one eye crossed out in yellow, a ringed eye beside it, and a grin of white teeth, drawn in dense purple, red and yellow scribble with a cloud of black loops above for hair. It is the most worked drawing in the Scrimshaw collection, with 320 recorded strokes, and the crossed eye is drawn as a yellow X in a red ring. May 11, 2021: an eye cancelled with an X, a grin, and a head buried in scribble, five months before nnxyz arrived on the board. The gallery does not say nnxyz saw it. It says it was there.

**Scrimshaw 33** (line) Two heads on black, drawn in pink, purple and yellow line, facing each other, with a red loop between them like a handle.

## Returns, 2023

Four works from August to December 2023, two and a half years after the rest: a dark head with a red mouth, a face with a third eye, a brown face on black, and a crowned skull. The collector bought each within days of its appearing.

**Scrimshaw 40** (line) A dark head on grey with green hair, a crossed eye, a red mouth of teeth, and colored scribble inside the face, drawn in black and purple line; the collector bought it the day after it appeared, in August 2023.

**Wobbler** (label) A face on teal with one ringed eye at the left, a blue eye at the right, a third eye drawn on the forehead in a diamond, and a mouth of black bars, drawn in orange, pink and blue line over a soft ground. A black hook hangs from the left ear. The title is Wobbler, and the face leans to the left as if it might. Drawn in October 2023, when nnxyz was drawing the numbered masks a few a month and the board was quiet; the collector bought it ten days later. The third eye is new. The mouth of bars is the old one.

**Scrimshaw 41** (line) A brown face on black drawn in white and black loops, two ringed eyes and a third ring above, a mouth of white zigzag teeth, in seventy-two strokes.

**Scrimshaw 42** (label) A skull in profile on black, facing left, drawn in pink, cyan, yellow and red line, with a yellow crown floating above it and a ringed eye, its teeth a row of yellow and orange bars. The line is thick and clean, every color separate. It is the last work in the Scrimshaw collection, from December 14, 2023, and where the first face was one blue line on a pale ground, this one is a dozen colors on black. A crowned skull in profile on black is also, almost exactly, the collection piece of New Nifty on the Block, drawn fourteen months earlier by the other artist. The collector bought it three days later. The gallery notes the rhyme and leaves the two artists to it.

## Closing

Look again at the first face, one blue line over pale color, drawn on a February afternoon in 2021, and then at the crowned skull on black from December 2023. The line got thicker and the ground got darker. The loops stayed. The last head wears a crown, like the last nnxyz masks of 2022, and the gallery leaves it at that: two artists, one board, one mouth, and a collector who hung them in the same house.
