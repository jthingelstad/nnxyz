# Modelled in Light (nifty-faces--light)

Spoken words: 1689, about 11.3 minutes.

## Introduction

Faces with weight, and for the first time in the collection, faces with someone behind them. The twenty works in this room are painted, not outlined: the cheek catches light, the eye sits in a socket, the chin casts a shadow. They come in two runs. In the winter of 2022 a set of quiet portraits, pale and soft, drawn in loops over discs of color. In the spring a set of glossy sculpted heads on flat bright grounds, shining like polished wood. The winter faces are the tenderest work in the Faces. The spring heads are the mask coming back, with a shine on it.

The ground changes with the paint, and so does the distance. The winter faces float in haze, with no edge between the face and the air, and three strokes in four are translucent, the most in the whole wing. You are close to them, the way you are close to someone across a table. The spring heads stand on one color, teal, orange, cyan, and their edges are hard. You are looking at an object on a shelf.

In the winter portraits the eyes look at you, or down, or away, and the mouths are closed. In the spring heads the eyes narrow, cross and hollow out, and the mouth starts to sneer. The glossy heads were drawn in the weeks the crypto market broke: the Terra collapse of May 9 to 13, 2022 falls between the mask with yellow beads and the head caught in the beams. The drawings do not mention it. They do not have to. This is the room where the collection shows what it can do when it stops performing, and then, as the money around it fell, decides to perform again, harder.

## Faces in the Glow

December 2021 and January 2022. A pale head in orange light, a face on black, and two portraits in haze. The paint arrives, and with it the first faces that are not laughing or biting.

**#49** (label) A pale head in a glow of orange and red, its eyes crooked, one higher than the other, its teeth hanging from the lower edge of the face like a row of keys. The head is drawn in white, green and pink loops over soft discs of color, and the light seems to come from behind it. It is the first painted face in the series, from December 6, 2021, and the one that sets the room's method: discs of soft color first, then loops of line. The crooked eyes and the hanging teeth should make it a monster. The glow makes it a saint. This is the first face in the collection that asks for sympathy instead of a laugh.

**xmas special** (line) A face on black with a white band across the brow like a cap, a thick mustache, and a mouth open on white teeth, painted in purple, orange and blue on Christmas Eve, a card from someone who does not do cards.

**#58** (label) A ghostly face in pale grey and lilac, with two dark hollow eyes and a small mouth, drawn in thin loops over discs of white and pink. The hair is pale strokes falling at the sides. Nothing is outlined. The face is made only of where the discs are lighter and darker, and the line on top is thin and loose. Three quarters of its strokes are translucent and it carries sixty-three colors, more than any other work in the wing. All of that effort goes into a face that is barely there, with the eyes of someone who has not slept. It is the first picture in the Faces that is sad on purpose.

**#61** (line) A woman with blue eyes and a small mouth, painted in olive and brown over soft discs, her hair loose at the sides, looking out at you with the plainest, most direct gaze in the wing.

## Quiet Portraits

January 26 to March 25, 2022. Eight faces in shadow: a bald man, a profile in loops, a lace collar, a hat and goggles, a man with his eyes closed. The stillest weeks in the collection.

**#62** (label) A bald man's head in dark brown and gold, with green eyes that look off to one side, a straight nose and a closed mouth. The light falls from the left, so the brow and the near cheek are gold and the far side of the face sinks into dark. A thin orange line follows the eyes and the lips. Four strokes in five are translucent, the most in the room. It is the stillest face in the series, and the most like a person seen in a room. He is not performing anything. He is waiting, and the room waits with him. If the collection has a single picture of patience, it is this one.

**#65** (line) A profile in loose white and black loops on cream, facing right, with an orange mouth and no teeth, forty-four strokes over soft discs of pink and lilac, dripping at the chin, a face half remembered.

**#66** (line) A dark head on white, tilted back with the chin raised, modelled in brown and black with red, green and white loops over it, its eyes narrowed: the one face in these weeks that looks down at you, and does not think much of what it sees.

**#69** (line) A long-haired figure in olive with dark eyes and a white lace collar, the collar drawn as a net of white lines and dots, the hair falling straight: a formal portrait, with the sitter's eyes lost in shadow and the collar doing all the talking.

**#85** (line) A grey head in a hat with two huge pale eyes like goggles, a grin of white teeth, modelled in grey and purple, the cartoon grin dropped into the painted room and looking slightly lost there.

**#100** (none) (title only)

**#103** (line) A man with his eyes closed, painted in brown and grey with orange and white line, on a ground of grey stripes, the one face in the Faces that has shut its eyes to the viewer, and the room's most private picture.

**#116** (line) A dark face on magenta with a mop of pink and gold hair and a red X in one eye, outlined in thick white, its mouth a grid of small teeth: the X eye and the grille are back, and the room's quiet is over.

## Glossy Heads

April 13 to May 22, 2022. Eight heads that shine: sculpted in dark paint, lit from one side, on flat bright grounds. The people are gone and the objects are back, polished and grinning.

**#121** (label) A glossy dark head on violet with one yellow eye and a wide grin of white teeth, drawn in white and black line over paint that shines. A row of white teeth sits on top of the head like a crown. The nose is a long triangle. It is the first of the glossy heads, from April 13, 2022, and it casts a shadow on the violet, the first shadow in the room. The shadow is what makes it an object. After the winter's faces, which had no edges, this one has a hard edge, a shine and a crown of teeth, and the grin is the box-head grin, back on a head that has been carved and varnished.

**#124** (line) A glossy head on orange with narrowed eyes and a sneer, painted in brown, gold and black with scribble over the crown, the first sneer in the Faces, and it suits the shine.

**#125** (line) A glossy dark head on lavender with a yellow cross in one eye and a target in the other, a grin of white teeth, and fingers resting on top of the head, as if someone were holding the mask up to show it.

**#126** (label) A glossy dark mask on a ground of orange and pink stripes, with two swirl eyes, a long nose, a row of large white teeth and a border of yellow beads around the head. The paint is black with white and blue highlights, and the beads are small yellow circles drawn at even spacing. The stripes behind it run in waves. With 372 recorded strokes it is the most worked of the glossy heads. It is a mask in the plain sense: a carved face with beads around the edge and nothing behind it, and the swirl eyes stare because they cannot do anything else. The winter faces looked at you. This one is for looking at.

**#129** (none) (title only)

**#130** (line) A glossy green head in a hat with flowers on the brim, on olive, with small eyes and an open mouth, a mask dressed for a wedding.

**#131** (line) A glossy face on cyan with crooked eyes, one high and one low, a row of white teeth, and a crown of yellow scribble for hair, the crooked eyes of the first painted head back in April, with the softness gone.

**#132** (label) A glossy dark head on orange, lit by pale beams from the upper left, its mouth open on white teeth, one eye a white ring in shadow. The beams are soft wedges of pale yellow laid over the head. Five months after the pale head in the orange glow, the light is back, but now it falls on something solid and casts a shadow. The last work in the room, from May 22, 2022, and the last of the numbered Faces, drawn nine days after Terra's collapse and five weeks after the collector bought his first nnxyz ink. The first painted face dissolved in the light. This one is caught in it, mouth open, like something found with a torch. That is the distance the series travelled in five months: from a face the light passes through to a face the light catches.

## Closing

The pale head in the orange glow is lit from behind, and the face is barely solid. The dark head from May is lit by beams, and it is all solid. In both, the light is the subject. In the first it dissolves the face, and what is left is gentle. In the last it strikes it, and what is left is a mask with its mouth open. Between them sit eight people who were simply painted, and the room is quietest where it is best.
