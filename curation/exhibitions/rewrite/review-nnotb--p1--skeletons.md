# Skeletons and Monsters (nnotb--p1--skeletons)

Spoken words: 1564, about 10.4 minutes.

## Introduction

Two kinds of body share this room. One is a skeleton: a stick figure of bones, drawn in a few colored lines, dancing or walking on a flat ground. The other is a monster: a hunched, furred or armored creature in the dark, with fangs. Twenty works, from New Year's Day 2022 to June 3. The skeletons are the series at play. The monsters are what it drew when it stopped playing.

The skeletons are all limbs. Their arms and legs bend at too many joints, their hands are spread like rakes, and their heads are small skulls with teeth. Most of them wear something: a jacket, a tutu, a hat. They are dead and they are dancing, and the joke never wears out. The monsters are all mass. They crouch, they fill the square, and the light picks out teeth and eyes and leaves the rest in shadow. The artist painted them with the care the skeletons never asked for, and the care is where the dread comes from.

This is also the room where the collecting began. Jamie Thingelstad's first purchase from the series was the skeleton that opens it, the horned figure in a jacket from New Year's Day, bought on May 1, 2022, for twenty xDai. Four days later he bought the blue bull walking through fire. The two kinds do not alternate neatly. Watch the stick figures loosen through February, then see the monsters take over in April and May, until the last work is a creature hanging in a dark forest.

## New Year Skeletons

Three stick figures from the first three days of 2022, then the first monster at the end of the month. The first of them became the collector's first purchase from the series.

**#16** (label) A horned figure in a jacket stands square on an olive ground, drawn in black with a cream fill, its ribs showing through the coat like a fossil. Thin red and yellow lines wander over the whole square in loose rectangles, as if a second drawing had been laid on the first. The hands are two rakes of fingers. The year 2022 begins with this work, made in the morning of January 1, and four months later it began something else: on May 1, 2022, it was the first New Nifty on the Block that Jamie Thingelstad bought, for twenty xDai, seventeen days after his first nnxyz purchase of any kind. A skeleton in a jacket, standing its ground with its ribs showing, was the one he chose first.

**#17** (line) A stick figure in a pink tutu stands on one leg like a pole, arms out, its head a ball of red scribble with two round eyes.

**#18** (line) A lanky figure in blue and orange line dances on teal, one arm raised high in a loop, its small skull grinning with white fangs.

**#26** (label) A skeleton painted in dark purple and grey emerges from black, its skull tilted toward you, its ribcage open, one hand held up with the fingers spread. Light catches the top of the skull, the knuckles and the edges of the ribs, and everything else sinks into the ground. It is the first painted body in the room, and the first with no color at all beyond the purple of the bone. The stick skeletons of the first week were dancing. This one has stopped, turned, and raised a hand, and the raised hand is the whole difference: a joke about death, painted with enough care, is no longer a joke.

## February: Hats and Fangs

Eight works in three weeks. Skeletons put on hats and cowboy boots, and between them the monsters arrive, one with fangs and one with a grin.

**#27** (label) A hunched creature with a long skull, a red mouth of white fangs and two red eyes crouches in grey light, its fur drawn in a halo of black and white scratches. Four days after the purple skeleton, the painted body is back, but furred, with hands planted on the ground and a spine of spikes down its back. The dark around it is not flat; it is made of soft grey discs, like a fog. The mouth is the only red thing, and it is open. It is the first monster in the series that is plainly angry, and it arrived on February 2, 2022, with nothing in the record to explain it except the drawing itself.

**#28** (line) Two skeletons in yellow and pink line meet on orange, one bending low and the other reaching down, their skulls grinning with white fangs.

**#30** (line) A skeleton in a tall yellow hat stands on teal, its boxy body striped with pink, red and yellow, both arms held out and its ribs drawn in white.

**#31** (line) A cowboy skeleton strides to the left on orange, hat tipped, one leg raised, its chest a box of blue and red scribble with a heart in it.

**#32** (line) A hunched figure in yellow, pink and blue scribble crouches on olive, its boxy head open at the front with two staring eyes and a row of teeth.

**#33** (line) A purple figure with a round head and a wide grin of white teeth holds out its arms in the dark, its eyes two red X marks, the grin and the cancelled eyes together for the first time in the room.

## Wings, Crowns and Cowboys

March and April. A moth, a crowned skeleton on a maze, a horned creature, and two more cowboys, one dancing on red and one walking downhill.

**#39** (label) A moth with wings of gold and black stripes spreads them across a purple ground, its small red face at the top with two antennae. The body is a stack of striped segments, and the wings are drawn in long curved strokes that follow their shape. Two round tips hang below like feet. It is the one winged creature in the room, and the most carefully built, with 380 recorded strokes. It was made on March 2, 2022, the day the artist posted a list of reasons for loving Nifty Ink: easy and fun, works without problems, no cost to mint, a nice and active community. The moth is that mood with wings on. It is also drawn to a lamp.

## Monsters

The last four works, from April 25 to June 3, are all monsters, and all painted. The skeletons are gone. The second of them, the bull, was bought the day it was drawn.

**#57** (label) A blob of a monster fills the square from the black, its mouth a cave of white teeth, its two eyes on stalks with orange lids. The body is drawn in green and brown with scribble over it, and a hand at the upper right holds up a small round thing. In February a purple figure grinned with a row of teeth; here the grin has become the whole body, and the eyes have left the head. It was drawn on April 25, 2022, the same day as the two cats with a heart between them in the room next door, and the same day the artist gave the collector the drawing that marked two hundred inks. One hand and one day made a love note and this.

**#66** (line) A blue bull with small horns walks through orange fire, its back and legs drawn in blue and green scribble, a bouquet of smoke rising from its tail; the collector bought it on May 5, 2022, the day after it was drawn, for fifteen xDai, four days before the market fell.

**#128** (line) A furry creature with a huge mane of colored strokes grins with white fangs, one red eye showing, on a ground of yellow circles.

**#138** (label) A hunched creature with long hair and long thin arms hangs in a dark forest, drawn in black and gold strokes between tall black tree trunks, the sky behind it burning orange. Its face looks down and is barely there. The hands reach toward the ground. It is the last work in the room, made June 3, 2022, and the only one where the monster has a place to be: a forest, with trees and a sky, and the sky is on fire. After five months of skeletons that danced and monsters that roared, the series ends its first part with a creature that has gone quiet and is looking at the ground. Whatever the fire behind it is, it has turned its back on it.

## Closing

Look again at the first skeleton, standing square in its jacket on New Year's Day, and then at the creature hanging in the forest in June. The one is all bone and line on a flat ground, and it is a joke about being dead. The other is all hair and shadow in a place with trees, and it is not a joke. The room walked from one to the other in five months, and the collector's first two purchases from the series, the New Year skeleton and the bull in the fire, stand at the two ends of the walk.
