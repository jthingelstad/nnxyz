# Occasions and Figures (nnxyz-other--ot-occasions)

Spoken words: 2292, about 15.3 minutes.

## Introduction

Pieces made for a moment. The works in this room were drawn for occasions: the hundredth, two hundredth, three hundredth and five hundredth inks, a summer, a pizza day, a greeting. With them hang the few full figures and painted scenes from outside the named series. Twenty-two works, from the artist's first day on Nifty Ink in October 2021 to December 2024, the last drawing in the collection. This is the artist in public.

Most of them carry words. NIFTY INK on a tee shirt, WAGMI on a bottle, a number held up like a sign, slogans written across a face. The words are drawn, not typed, and they sit in the picture like any other mark. The milestones were never for sale: 200 nifties was minted straight into the wallets of collectors who had bought the inks, Jamie Thingelstad's among them, on April 25, 2022, the first nnxyz work he was given, and 300 nifties followed the same way in June. The artist celebrated by handing the celebration out.

Watch the sun-head. It appears in May 2022 as a figure with a sun for a face, in a NIFTY INK tee shirt, on a beach, in the dark, dancing, and the collector bought all three for a single xDai each. Two years later it comes back as a pizza. The room ends on two masks covered in writing from December 2024: dishwasher to memewasher, blue chip, sold by Christie's, no need for a wall. By then the artist had been posting for a year about the market as a rare-token scheme that had nothing to do with art, and these are the drawings that say it.

## The First Day and a Dragon

October 20, 2021, the first day of nnxyz on Nifty Ink: a figure in a cap and a river landscape. Then, in December, a dragon on the water. The collector bought the first two on August 11, 2022, the same day he bought most of the Scrimshaws, and received the dragon as a gift with the last Rawblocks in March 2023.

**randoomdle with cap** (label) A figure in a blue cap and a yellow shirt, drawn in orange and white hatching with the letters N N X Y Z written in pink across its face, grinning with white teeth. The ground is a dense weave of red, green and blue hatching. The first nnxyz work on Nifty Ink, drawn October 20, 2021, and the name is already on the face. The artist's handle on every platform was four zeros and these five letters, and the first drawing wears them as a mask. Jamie Thingelstad bought it on the evening of August 11, 2022, for twenty xDai, the same night he bought the river below it and most of the Scrimshaws, and two nights before he bought the Faces origin piece. It was the week the artist announced the burning. The collector's answer was to go back to the beginning.

**good morning nifty randoomdle** (label) A river runs through a green valley under a sky of pink and white cloud, with a tree at the left and a stump at the right, all painted in soft strokes and scribble. Drawn six hours after the figure in the cap, on the same first day. It is a landscape without a face in it, and with 737 recorded strokes one of the most worked drawings in the wing. On the first day the artist drew the name, and then a river. The two landscapes that close the Rawblocks, seventeen months later, are this picture again, and this is where the collector started too: he bought it on August 11, 2022, for fifteen xDai, six hours before the cap.

**Smaug on the Water** (label) A dragon rises from blue water, its head at the right, its body a long curve of gold and white strokes, its tail trailing across the dark sky. The water is a mass of cyan and white scribble like foam. The sky is black. It is the most densely drawn work in the whole collection, and the only dragon. It was drawn on December 7, 2021, the day before New Nifty on the Block began, and the artist sent it to the collector's wallet on March 28, 2023, a fortnight after the last handover, with the drawing for five hundred inks: two gifts, after everything else had gone, as if to finish the set. The dragon's name is Tolkien's. The artist, who never named an influence, named this one.

## Hundreds and Summers

January to November 2022. Four milestone drawings for the hundredth, two hundredth, three hundredth and five hundredth inks, and three summer figures with a sun for a head. Two of the milestones were gifts to the collector; the summer figures cost one xDai each.

**100 nifties** (label) Three cartoon characters in green, blue and pink stand in a row with the letters X, Y and Z on their chests and the number 100 drawn above them in pale balloon letters. Their eyes are wide, their mouths open, and they drip at the feet into a grey floor. The milestone for the hundredth ink, made January 28, 2022, and the artist posted that it had been dropped into the pockets of some niftians. The three letters are the artist's name split into three people, and they are melting. The collector bought a copy in November 2022 for five xDai, after the series had ended, to have the first of the milestones.

**200 nifties** (line) Figures glow in a burst of light, drawn in gold and grey with a hand raised, and the words 200 NIFTY INK at the upper left; minted into the collector's wallet on April 25, 2022, the first nnxyz work he was given.

**nifty summer ink** (label) A figure with a sun for a head stands on blue in a white tee that says NIFTY INK, black shorts and sunglasses with X Y Z on the lenses, holding a bottle labeled WAGMI with bubbles rising from it. The sun is yellow with short rays. The arms and legs are yellow lines. The first of three summer figures, drawn May 10, 2022, the day after the market fell, and posted with a joke about Nifty Ink as a no-fuss printer. A sun in sunglasses with a bottle of champagne is a picture of a good mood held up against the news, and the collector bought it the morning it appeared, for one xDai, his first nnxyz purchase outside the Faces, three weeks after the first.

**nifty summer night ink** (line) The same sun-headed figure at night, glowing in a ring of yellow on black, its tee grey, its arms out, bought the same evening for one xDai.

**nifty summer dance ink** (line) The sun-headed figure dances on teal in headphones, one leg kicked out, the word WAGMI floating around it with music notes and wavy lines, the third for one xDai, two days later.

**300 nifties** (line) A figure in black and white line with a visor and a grin holds a line that runs up to the signature, and a small figure at its feet holds up a sign that says 300; minted into the collector's wallet on June 20, 2022, with many others.

**500 inks** (label) A face in dense colored line on orange: a long nose, two glasses-like rings, dark hair falling at the sides, a small closed mouth, drawn in black, yellow, blue, white and red strokes over a painted face. It is the milestone for the five hundredth ink, drawn November 12, 2022, and it is a portrait, not a cartoon. The first three milestones were cartoons and this one is a tired person with their mouth shut, drawn the day after FTX filed for bankruptcy, in the week of the Rawblocks portraits. The artist sent it to the collector in March 2023, with the dragon. Five hundred drawings in, the celebration is a face that has stopped celebrating.

## Figures of 2023 and 2024

Six numbered works from November 2023 to March 2024 that are figures, not masks: a dancing girl, a rabbit, a hooded figure, a crouching creature, a pair, and a creature with hearts. After the series ended, the artist was still drawing for an audience that had mostly left, and the collector was still there.

**#8** (line) A girl with orange curly hair and pink cheeks dances in a teal frame, her arms drawn in blue loops, a shower of yellow and blue dots falling at the left.

**#12** (label) On pink, a black figure walks at the left, a red rabbit stands at the center with ringed eyes, and a dark figure with a white smile stands at the right under a yellow sun with a face. Yellow bars float behind them. The three figures are drawn in different ways, the first in black with a yellow face, the rabbit in red scribble, the third in black with a tangle of yellow line. It is the one crowd scene in the room, drawn December 28, 2023, in a run of eight drawings in three days. A year after the series ended, three figures walking in the sun on pink: the block, with three people left on it.

**#18** (line) A hooded figure painted in dark brown against orange and gold, its face lost in the hood, thin lines ruled across the whole square.

**#26** (line) A black creature with a crown of spikes and a grin of teeth crouches on yellow, drawn in black and blue scribble, hatched lines at the right.

**#34** (line) Two figures on teal, one in a cap and one with red X eyes and a red beam, drawn in dark scribble with white line over them.

**#35** (label) A green and red creature in sunglasses with a mouth of teeth stands on purple, with three red hearts beside it, each with a small face, and a dark figure in a cap on a pole at the right. A sad sun looks down from the top. Blue raindrops fall. The hearts have faces, the sun has a face, and the creature has sunglasses: everything in the picture looks at something. It was drawn on March 20, 2024, two days before the artist posted that success in our society is not tied to hard work. Three hearts with worried faces, a sad sun, rain, and a creature in sunglasses grinning through it: the picture is a mood, and the mood is the post.

## Pizza Day and After

May to December 2024. Three pizza drawings in two days, a pink head, and two masks covered in words. The pizza drawings were bought for one and two xDai.

**pizza pizza day** (label) A pizza slice for a head, with red glasses and a grin, under a yellow sun with a face, drawn in orange and gold hatching with yellow rays over everything. Two years after the sun-headed summer figures, the sun is back, and the figure under it has become a slice of pizza. The first of three pizza drawings, made May 19, 2024, two years after the first summer figure. Bitcoin Pizza Day, May 22, is the anniversary of the first thing ever bought with the coin, and the artist, who had spent the month posting that the market was a scheme, drew pizza for two days ahead of it, a grinning slice in glasses first. The collector bought all three for one or two xDai each.

**pizza pizza pizza day** (line) A round head on black with pizza slices on top like a crown, blue glasses, a grin of white teeth, and a slice of pizza on its white shirt, the crown of the last masks made of pizza.

**pizza pizza pizza pizza day** (line) A man in red glasses and a red cape stands in blue rain holding up a sign with hearts, a pizza flying at the upper right and a small creature at his feet.

**abi ninja** (none) (title only)

**#64** (label) A face inside a black frame, covered in words: DIGITAL ART in pink, BLUE CHIP in blue, OG, and a line about going from dishwasher to memewasher along the top. The face has a red crossed eye, a grin of white teeth and a yellow hook of a nose. Green and blue scribble fills the rest. The words are drawn in as many hands as colors. Drawn December 4, 2024, after seven months away from the board. They are the words of the market the artist had been writing against all year: rich, rich, rich; blue chip; OG. The face wearing them has one eye crossed out and is grinning, and the frame around it is the wall the next drawing says there is no need for.

**#66** (label) A mask with a pink flower for an eye and a grin of teeth, under the words NO NEED FOR A WALL in yellow and black, with memewasher, artfluencer and sold by Christie's written around it in white, pink and red. Drawn the day after the framed face, December 5, 2024. It is the last work in the collection by date, and it is mostly writing. A flower for an eye is the gentlest eye in the collection, and the mask that wears it is surrounded by the vocabulary of the trade: influencer, auction house, meme. No need for a wall is the artist's last sentence on the board as far as this collection goes, and it can be read two ways, as a boast about digital art and as a shrug about galleries. This gallery takes it as both, and hangs it on a wall.

## Closing

Look again at the first figure, with the artist's name written across its face in October 2021, and then at the last, a mask under a slogan in December 2024. Three years apart, and both are faces made partly of words. The first says who the artist is. The last says what the artist thought of the business the first one walked into, and both are grinning.
