# Skulls and Shadows (nifty-faces--dark)

Spoken words: 1753, about 11.7 minutes.

## Introduction

Fear, drawn as a face. The twenty works in this room share a ground of black or near-black, and out of it the drawing pulls a head: a halo of ochre, a white line, a pair of lit eyes, a grille of teeth. They run from December 2021 to April 2022, and December was the darkest month of the whole collection, with two grounds in three laid down black.

These are the skulls and the hooded things, and the room does not pretend they are anything else. But look at how they are made and the fear turns out to be a game the artist is playing with you. In some works the black is a flat ground and the head is a few white lines on it, a skull in twenty-six strokes with the artist's own name written where its eyes should be. In others the black is paint, and a head is modelled out of it with highlights until it shines like a lamp in a cellar. The first kind is a cartoon of death. The second is a portrait of it.

Watch the teeth. They are nearly the only white in most of these pictures and they are what the eye finds first: a zipper, a grille, four fangs, a row of pegs hanging from a jaw. Every one of these mouths is a grin and a threat at once, and the room never says which. That is the flaw the artist built in on purpose, and it is why these faces keep you looking.

## Heads in the Dark

Four works from December 4 to 6, 2021, the week the dark grounds begin. A head in an ochre halo, a black head on white, a blue head lit from inside, and a face in a pink frame.

**#36** (label) A head on black inside a halo of ochre, with two target eyes in yellow rings, a white zigzag along the brow, and a mouth of white teeth drawn like a zipper. Inside the head the fill is colored scribble, cyan, pink and yellow, with black line over it. The halo is the only soft thing in the picture, a band of brown that keeps the head from falling into the black. This is the series' first dark work, and the zipper mouth is the whole room in one mark: it is a smile until you notice it is also closed.

**#37** (line) A black head on a white ground, one ringed eye and one boxed eye, a long nose, and a grille of grey teeth in a red mouth, the one head here that stands in daylight and is no less a skull for it.

**#38** (label) A dark blue head with two yellow slit eyes and a grin of white teeth, painted in blue and black with white highlights along the crown. Half its strokes are translucent, more than any other work in the room, and the light they build comes from the upper left, picking out the brow and leaving the jaw in shadow. The eyes glow in their sockets like something awake in a dark room. The grin curves up at the right. It is the first painted head in the Faces that shines, and the first that watches you back.

**#44** (line) A face in a frame of pink loops on dark red, one eye a sun of orange rays and the other an arched box, with a grille of white teeth at the bottom: a shrine with a mouth.

## Skulls and Hoods

January 21 to February 9, 2022. Seven works in three weeks, and the room's two ways of drawing fear side by side: skulls in a few strokes of line, and hooded figures with almost no face at all.

**#56** (line) A skull drawn in yellow and orange loops over a curtain of dark vertical strokes, three quarters of them translucent, so that the ghost is mostly veil and the teeth are what is solid.

**#59** (line) A face drawn in white and red line on dark red, with X marks in both eyes, spiky lashes and a small mouth of fangs, in forty-seven strokes: the eyes are cancelled and the mouth is still open.

**#63** (label) A tall head on dark teal with two starburst eyes under spiky lashes, a green cross for a nose, and a mouth of five white teeth in a box. The head is drawn in white, orange and cyan line over soft vertical strokes of blue and grey, so it stands in rain. The two eyes are different, a white disc at the left and an orange ring at the right, and the mismatch gives the face the look of someone caught mid-thought. The cross on the nose is drawn in green zigzag. Of all the heads in the room this is the one that is not afraid and not frightening; it is just standing there, getting wet.

**#71** (label) A black mass of hair on grey, and in it a face: two white eyes with dark pupils, a short line for a nose, and four white fangs. Twenty-three recorded strokes, the fewest in the room. The hair is drawn as a few loops of white line over black, and the black is one shape. The whole face is the eyes and the fangs, and they are enough. This is fear at its cheapest and most effective, the thing under the bed drawn by someone who knows exactly how little it takes.

**#72** (label) A hooded skull on black, drawn in white line with a red hood, and the letters N N X Y Z written in red across its brow and its eyes. Two rows of white teeth sit below. Two hands rest at the bottom in red outline. Twenty-six strokes. The letters are the artist's name, and they sit on the skull where the eyes should be, so the name does the looking. Death in a hood is the oldest cartoon there is, and the artist signs it across the face: this is me, and I am grinning.

**#73** (line) A hooded figure on black, its hood and shoulders outlined with yellow dots like a fairground sign, a white face with one sun-burst eye and one red star eye, and a grille of white teeth in a red box.

**#74** (line) A hooded head painted in orange and brown, with no face at all except a wide grin of white teeth in the dark of the hood, and the grin is enough to make the dark a face.

## Beams and Blue Eyes

February 12 to 21, 2022. A horned head, a skull with pink eyes lit by beams, a skull with blue eyes that look wet, and a bird skull. The beams that will light the whole of May arrive here first.

**#84** (line) A horned dark head on green, two tall white eyes under a heavy brow and a grille of yellow teeth, drawn in black and blue scribble over soft green blobs, a devil who looks more worried than wicked.

**#88** (label) A skull with two huge pink eyes, each a ring of magenta rays around a white center, lit by pale beams that cross the square from the upper left. The skull is drawn in black and colored line over dark, and the mouth is a row of white triangle teeth in a dark box. The beams are the first in the Faces, soft bands laid over everything, and where they fall the lines show through them. Three months later they will light nearly every drawing in May. Here they fall on a skull, and the skull looks dazzled, not dead.

**#90** (label) A skull painted in pink, brown and grey on black, with two blue eyes in dark sockets, each a ring of blue with a white highlight, and a row of white teeth hanging from the jaw at the lower right. Nothing is outlined; two strokes in three are translucent and the skull is modelled from them with a thin white edge. Where the blue head of December glowed from inside, this one is lit from the front, and the eyes look wet. A skull cannot cry, and this one looks as if it is about to. It is the saddest picture in the room, and the most carefully painted.

**#94** (line) A bird skull with a long beak, drawn in black, white, red and cyan strokes on dark brown, a brush of pale lines hanging from the jaw like a beard: the only skull here that is not a person.

## Spring Skulls

March 16 to April 25, 2022. Five heads, and the dark ground is now a stage: a box with an X eye, a chunky head with a heart for an eye, a head of blocks, a glossy bald head screaming, and a skull with its jaw off.

**#107** (none) (title only)

**#108** (line) A chunky head on dark grey with a red heart in a box for an eye, a box of white teeth, and two more boxes of teeth at the bottom like feet, in confetti scribble: the heart is crossed with a yellow bar, and the head is cheerful anyway.

**#112** (line) A head built from stacked blocks on dark navy, one eye in a tilted box and the other a cluster of rings, a long bar of white teeth at the bottom, a face assembled like a machine and worried like a person.

**#122** (line) A glossy bald head on black, its skin a weave of colored scribble, two ringed eyes, and a mouth thrown open on white teeth, one side of the head lit white: the room's one scream, and it is drawn in party colors.

**#128** (label) A glossy skull on pink with spiky hair, one huge yellow eye, and its jaw come off, lying below the teeth as a separate row. The skull is painted in gold and black with white highlights, and the teeth hang from the upper jaw in a row of long pegs. Two months after the skull with blue eyes, the modelled skull is back, and this time it has come apart. The pink ground is the brightest in the room and the picture is the room's most broken. The last work here, from April 25, 2022, and the grin has finally fallen off the face.

## Closing

Look again at the hooded skull with the letters across its eyes, twenty-six strokes on black, and then at the glossy skull on pink with its jaw on the floor. One is nothing but line, and it is laughing at you. The other is modelled, lit and broken, and it is not. Both are skulls in the dark, and both make you look at the teeth, which is where this collection decides whether a face is joking.
