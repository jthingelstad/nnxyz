# Cats and Creatures (nnotb--p3--cats)

Spoken words: 1866, about 12.4 minutes.

## Introduction

Cats, and then creatures. For two weeks at the end of August 2022 nearly every drawing was a cat: black, boxy, big-eared, standing or sitting in a striped room with its teeth showing. Then, in the first days of September, the cats gave way to flat single creatures, each one color on one color. Twenty-six works, August 20 to September 14. The cats of the spring had been silhouettes with spiral eyes; these are built like the summer's masks, with bodies, and they have come indoors.

The cats are made from boxes, rings and grilles, but they have rooms to stand in. Stripes run behind them like wallpaper or a fence. Two of them share one drawing, and that drawing exists twice, published 85 seconds apart, the second time with a signature. They were drawn in the first fortnight of the burning: on August 23 the artist repeated that all four hundred inks would gradually be burned, and that everything after that was part of a new story. The next day the price of a new ink dropped from fifteen xDai to five, and Jamie Thingelstad bought the two cats drawn that day, the seated one in the red room and the white one in the yellow room.

Watch the stroke count fall. The cats are drawn in two to five hundred strokes. The flat creatures that follow them take thirty to fifty, and the room ends on a scribbled horse that takes forty. A series that had spent the summer burying faces in line spent September drawing one creature at a time in one color, as if the burning had made the drawing lighter.

## The First Cats

August 20 to 24. Six cats in five days, from a crowned cat mask to a white cat on yellow. The day after the first of them, the artist minted a three-copy series on Ethereum called i was famous.

**#237** (label) A black cat mask with a yellow crown sits on a dark red body, on a ground of gold scribbled with yellow bars. One eye is a blue ring with a cross in it, the other a yellow ring with a Z. The mouth is a box of white teeth under a yellow zigzag. It is the first work after the mask summer, drawn on August 20, 2022, and it is both things at once: the last mask and the first cat. The artist posted it that day at twenty xDai, five more than the masks around it. A crowned black cat with one eye cancelled is the summer's mask given a body and a name, and the price says the artist thought so too.

**#240** (line) A black cat head with one red spiral eye and one blue, a grin of white teeth, and a crest of green, set in a weave of blue, yellow and black bars.

**#242** (line) A cat figure stands with one arm raised in a room of purple and blue stripes, two round yellow eyes with flowers in them, a box of teeth below.

**#243** (label) A black cat with two glowing yellow eyes and a wide grille of white teeth fills the square, its ears tall, its chest a black block over a red rug. The eyes are drawn as yellow rings with light inside them, and the teeth are a long bar with black between each one. Stripes of yellow, pink and white stand behind the cat like a curtain. With 395 recorded strokes it is the densest of the first cats. The glowing eyes are the May beams come back, and the grille of teeth is the summer's. This cat is sitting in the dark with the lights on, three days into the burning, and it does not look like it plans to leave.

## Two Cats, Twice

August 24 to 26. A seated cat in a red room, a white creature in a yellow one, a cat on cream, and a drawing of two cats that was published twice. The first two were bought by the collector the day they were drawn, for five xDai each, the new price.

**#246** (line) A black cat sits upright in a room of blue and red stripes, its face a white loop with a spiral eye, its body ruled with pink lines like a cage; bought by the collector the day it was drawn, August 24, for five xDai, a minute after the white cat.

**#247** (line) A white cat with a long neck and a red mouth sits in a yellow room, a small black cat at its feet and a bird on a perch at the upper left; bought the same evening, also for five, the first of the two.

**#248** (label) A black cat stands on a cream ground with a red X over each eye, its ears tipped in pink, a grin of white teeth, and a pink collar around its neck. Its tail rises in a hook. The room behind it is pale: a few blocks of orange, blue and grey, and thin lines running in loops. It is the quietest cat in the room, and the first with the X in both eyes. A cat with a collar is somebody's cat. The X in both eyes and the grin are the summer's, and the collar is new: of all the cats in the room this is the one that has a home, and it is the one that cannot see.

**#249** (label) Two black cats side by side, the left one with a red X over one eye and a blue ring in the other, the right one with a red X and a green box. A small blue cat sits between them on a shelf. Below them a maze of black boxes with white line, and a yellow spiral at the right edge. The drawing was published on August 25, 2022, without a signature. Two cats and a small one between them is a family, and the maze under them is the block.

**#249 signed** (label) The same drawing, published again 85 seconds later, with one addition: the yellow signature at the lower right. Everything else is the same, the two cats, the small blue cat, the maze. Nine more strokes were recorded, and they are the signature. Both were kept, and both hang here, because the record kept both: on Nifty Ink a drawing is published the moment it is saved, and the artist, who signed nearly everything, had forgotten the signature and went back for it. It is the smallest story in the collection and one of the truest: a minute and a half, and a name.

**#251** (line) A cat-eared mask with two ringed eyes full of green and pink line, a cage of white teeth in a red mouth, and yellow beads along the right edge, on pale blue.

## Last Cats

August 26 to 28. Two cats drawn in white, a dense scene with a bird, a scribbled cat on cream, and a cat robot in the dark.

**#252** (label) A white cat on black, its face a mass of pink, blue and yellow line, with a red X over one eye and a wide open eye on the other side, a blue beam coming in from the left, and a wide grin of white teeth. The ears are tall and striped. Blue and pink beads run down both sides. Its 437 recorded strokes are the most in the room. One eye cancelled and one eye wide open, with a beam hitting the face: it is the wing's whole argument in one cat. The grin is enormous and the open eye is terrified, and the drawing is honest enough to give both the same weight.

## Flat Creatures

August 28 to September 2, and one horse from September 14. The drawings empty out. A white coat and a black figure on purple, a brown figure on maroon, and then single creatures in one color on one color. The two crowned creatures leaning on each other were bought by the collector on August 30.

**#257** (label) Two figures on purple: a white coat with a red cap, drawn in a few black lines, and a small black figure with a cloud of curly hair and a yellow eye. Eighty-two recorded strokes. Two days after the white cat on black, with its hundreds of lines, the drawing has emptied out to two shapes and a flat ground. The white figure looks up and away; the small black one looks out. It is a picture of two people in a room not talking, and after a summer of masks it is the first drawing in the wing that is about two people.

**#268** (label) A black skull in profile on lavender, with an X for an eye and a few purple lines for the jaw and the teeth, ringed with short black wavy lines. A black oval lies below it like a shadow. It is the flat silhouette from the spring, back for one drawing, and the X that sat on the cats' eyes is now the only mark on the face. In April the same formula made a bear hugging its belly. On September 2, with the inks burning, it made a skull, and the wavy lines that once made a cat hum now make a skull hum, which is a different sound.

**#284** (line) Twelve days later, a horse drawn in yellow, orange and white scribble over a black body, walking on teal with its head down and a mane of white lines, the day before the Ethereum Merge.

## Closing

Look again at the first cat, crowned and packed to the edges of the square, and then at the skull in profile, one black shape on lavender. The room moved from hundreds of strokes to a few dozen in two weeks, while the artist burned the year's inks and sold the new ones for five xDai. The X stayed in the eye the whole way. The cats, like the masks, never stopped grinning, and the grin never settled whether it was pleased or in pain.
