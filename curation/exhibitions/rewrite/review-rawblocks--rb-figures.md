# Patterned Masks and Black Figures (rawblocks--rb-figures)

Spoken words: 1823, about 12.2 minutes.

## Introduction

Patterned masks, and then black figures. This room holds two things that look nothing alike and were drawn in the same two weeks of November 2022. First the masks: faces packed with pattern, hearts, hatching, spirals, crowns, drawn over paint until the square is full. Then the figures: flat black shapes on a bright ground, a girl in a dress, a creature on all fours, a man in the rain. The masks are the old vocabulary. The figures are something the series had not done before, and they came in the two days the exchange went under.

The two earliest works in the room are the two earliest in the whole series, from May 8 and 9, 2022, the weekend the Terra stablecoin began to fall, and they are masks too, packed to the frame. Jamie Thingelstad bought the first of them the day it was posted, his first Rawblocks, six months before the series properly began. The masks that follow in November turn into skulls and sun-faces, and then on November 10 the black figures arrive, three in one evening.

Watch the black. In the masks it is one color among many. In the figures it is the whole body, and everything else, the maze, the sun, the rain, is drawn around it. The girl with the flower was drawn on the evening of November 10. The man in the rain with the question marks was drawn on the morning of November 11, 2022, hours before FTX filed for bankruptcy. Nothing in the record says the artist drew the news. The drawings say what the week felt like.

## Two from May

May 8 and 9, 2022. The first two Rawblocks works, drawn six months before the rest: two dense masks inside white frames, made the weekend Terra began to fall. The collector bought the first the day it appeared.

**#1** (label) A mask packed with pattern inside a white frame: a ringed eye in a box at the left, a spiral eye in a box at the right, a mouth of white bars, and around them columns of beads, zigzags and hatching in red, yellow, blue and green. Nothing is empty. The first Rawblocks work, drawn May 8, 2022, with 387 recorded strokes, the most in the room. It was drawn on the Sunday the Terra stablecoin began to slip, between the beam heads of the other series, and the next day Jamie Thingelstad bought it for twenty xDai: the first Rawblocks anyone owned, bought on the day the market broke. The name of the series means what it says. A raw block is a square filled until it is solid.

**#2** (line) A second mask in a white frame, the next day, with a red target eye in a box, a gold eye in a leaf shape, and a wide mouth of white and red zigzag.

## Masks and Skulls

October 30 to November 9, 2022. Ten masks: a crowned face with a red X eye, a green skull, two pink faces with one eye each, a half-sun skull, a skull in red rays, and three on pink. The last six were drawn on November 9, the day the rescue of the exchange fell through.

**#4** (line) A mask with a crown of red spikes, a red X over one eye and a cross over the other, a snarl of white teeth, on stripes of yellow and teal, the crowned skull of October brought into the new series.

**#5** (line) A round green skull with two ringed eyes, one red and one blue, a crown of purple capsules, and a wide grin of white teeth, drawn over olive.

**#19** (label) A face in profile on pink with one huge eye ringed in yellow and pink lashes, a red pupil, blue tubes hanging under it, and a small red mouth. The face is painted in dark red and drawn over in white and pink. A black shadow lies along the bottom. It is the first of a pair: the same face returns an hour later with blue hair. One eye, enormous, with the lashes drawn one by one, and a mouth too small to speak with: it is a face that is all looking, drawn on November 7, in the middle of the week everyone was watching one thing.

**#20** (line) The same face an hour later, now with a crest of blue hair, a yellow flower in its mouth, and a yellow coat, on the same pink, and the flower makes it a joke.

**#24** (label) A skull split down the middle: the left half painted in soft yellow with a pale eye, the right half a gold sun-face with a spiral eye and a ring of yellow rays. A rope of gold runs down between them. The mouth is a grille of white teeth in a gold box. The ground is a haze of orange and purple discs. Two faces in one skull, one quiet and one burning. It was drawn on November 9, 2022, the day the deal to rescue the exchange collapsed. The quiet half and the burning half share a jaw, and the grin is the same on both sides. It is the wing's thesis in one head: the calm and the panic are one face.

**#25** (label) A skull mask in a field of red rays, drawn in white and blue line with a target eye crossed in red at the left and a spiral eye at the right, a grille of white teeth, and a crown of spikes. Bones cross below the jaw. The rays are short red strokes on dark grey, standing out from the skull on every side. Its 576 recorded strokes are the most of any mask in the room. A crowned skull with crossbones in a halo of red: the pirate flag and the saint's halo at once, drawn on November 9. The spiral eye is dizzy and the crossed eye is shut, and the rays say the thing is radiating something. On that day, it was probably not calm.

**#29** (line) A patterned mask on pink, drawn in loose loops of white, green, blue and yellow, with two ringed eyes and a mouth of white bars.

**#30** (line) A mask on pink with a red crown on a yellow head, a red spiral eye, a wheel eye, a rainbow mouth, and a ring of beads around the face.

**#31** (line) A round mask with a crest of orange spikes and a spiral eye stands on a stick body with two blue arms, on brown.

## Black Figures

November 10 and 11, 2022. A dark scribbled head, then nine flat black figures in two days: a girl, a creature in a maze, a tube figure, a ghost in a frame, a man in the rain. The exchange filed for bankruptcy on the 11th.

**#32** (line) A dark head on navy with cat ears, two red-rimmed eyes and a mouth of white teeth, drawn in colored scribble over black, the last scribbled head before the black figures.

**#33** (line) A black figure with spiky hair and yellow legs bends over in a tangle of yellow, white and pink pipes on pink.

**#34** (label) A black figure on all fours in a maze of yellow pipes on green, its spiky head a ball with a red X eye and a grin, and three small black figures standing on its back. The big figure is one flat black shape with round feet. The maze is drawn in thick yellow strokes that fill every gap. The flat black figures begin here, and the small ones on its back are drawn the same way at a tenth of the size. A creature on all fours in a maze, carrying three others, drawn on November 10, 2022: the picture does not need the date to be a picture of being lost and carrying people, but it has the date.

**#35** (none) (title only)

**#36** (label) A girl in a black dress with a white face and a ponytail stands in a teal frame, her hands white, a small red flower at her feet. The face is a white circle with two black eyes and an O mouth. The dress has a row of white buttons. Fifty-four strokes. A grey shadow falls behind her. It is the quietest figure in the room, and the only one with a flower. Her mouth is an O: she is surprised, or singing, or about to cry. She was drawn on the evening of November 10, 2022, with the exchange a day from bankruptcy and the artist posting that artists, unlike marketplaces, think they have no choice. A girl with a flower at her feet is the gentlest thing in the series, and it comes from the worst week.

**#37** (line) A black creature with yellow bands on its arms raises both hands in a teal frame, two white eyes with blue pupils, a grin, and a small black figure standing on its head.

**#38** (none) (title only)

**#39** (label) A black head in profile with spiky hair on olive, one white eye, and a white hand raised to its chin, inside a dark frame. Twenty-five strokes. The head is one black shape with a long shadow behind it, and the hand is white so it shows. It is the sparest work in the series, drawn on November 11, 2022, the day FTX filed for bankruptcy. The whole drawing is a person thinking. After a year of three hundred grins, the artist drew a head with its hand at its chin and no mouth at all, in twenty-five strokes, and it says more than most of the masks.

**#40** (line) A patterned mask on pink with goggles, two ringed eyes inside them, a crown of white spikes, and a mouth of white teeth, drawn in yellow and black line, the mask coming back for a moment between the figures.

**#41** (label) A black figure with a smiling X-eyed face and a red heart on its chest stands in blue rain under a yellow sun with a face, its arms out, two black hands rising at the bottom corners. Question marks float in clouds at the sides. It is drawn inside a cream frame. Where the girl in the teal frame stood still, this figure stands in weather, and the sun looks down at it with its own face. It was drawn on the morning of November 11, 2022, hours before the bankruptcy filing. The eyes are crossed out and the mouth is smiling and the heart is showing, and the clouds on either side say the one thing the whole week was saying: question mark, exclamation mark. It is the only drawing in the series with punctuation in it, and it needed it.

## Closing

Six months separate the first mask, packed to its frame with beads and bars, from the black head in profile with its white hand, drawn in twenty-five strokes. The whole range of the series is between them: everything, and almost nothing. The first was drawn the weekend one stablecoin collapsed, the second the day an exchange did, and the artist's answer to the second was a black figure thinking, with its hand at its chin.
