# Coda: Two Landscapes (rawblocks--rb-coda)

Spoken words: 535, about 3.6 minutes.

## Introduction

Two landscapes to end the wing. They were painted on March 15, 2023, in the two hours after the collection piece, and they are the last Rawblocks works. There is no head in either one. A sun sets through a stand of trees, and light falls through a forest onto a floor of fallen trunks. The artist had just finished a square with every creature of the series crowded into it, and then painted trees, twice, with more strokes than either of the series' origin pieces.

They hang here as a coda, apart from the rooms, because they look like nothing else in the series. The brushwork is the series' brushwork, soft discs of color with scribble and line over them, turned for once on trees and sky. That same evening the collector bought the collection piece and the series went to him, and in the days after, the unsold copies of the year's inks went to the burn address by the hundred. The last two things the artist painted before the fire were a sunset and a forest with the light coming through.

## Two Landscapes

Both painted on March 15, 2023, the sunset first and the forest half an hour later, in the two hours after the collection piece.

**#64** (label) A sunset through trees. A yellow sun blazes at the top with rays of pink, blue and yellow, and under it a stand of red-trunked trees with green crowns stands on a field of gold and orange, ruled with dark lines like a floor. The whole picture is painted in soft discs with scribble over them, and the sun's rays reach every corner. With 831 recorded strokes it is one of the two most worked drawings in the series. Ninety minutes after packing every creature of the year into the collection piece, the artist painted a sun with no face and trees with no one under them. In August the artist had written of a journey into the sun. This is the sun, reached, and it does not grin.

**#65** (label) Light through a forest. Tall dark trees stand at the sides and a beam of pale light falls between them onto a clearing, where fallen trunks lie in the foreground painted in brown and red. Soft discs of white and lilac float in the light. Half an hour after the sunset, the light has moved from the sky to the ground, and the picture is dark except where it falls. With 1,061 recorded strokes it is the most worked drawing in the Rawblocks, and the last. The beam is the searchlight of the whole collection, the one that lit the eyes in May and crossed the skulls in September, and here it falls on fallen trees in an empty wood. Nobody is in the picture. That is the last thing the series has to say, and it took more strokes than anything before it.

## Closing

The sun in the first picture is the sun-face of the series without its face. Look at it once more, then at the forest, where the light falls on nothing but trees. That is where the Rawblocks end: with the mask set down, the crowd handed over, and the artist looking at the light.
