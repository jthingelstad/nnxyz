# Square Masks (nnotb--p2--square)

Spoken words: 1747, about 11.6 minutes.

## Introduction

A head drawn as a box, with boxes inside it. That is the mask of this room: square eyes holding spirals, targets or crosses, a mouth drawn as a grille of upright bars, and every corner of the square filled with zigzag, beads and scribble. Twenty-four works, from June 8 to August 19, 2022. For ten weeks that summer nearly everything the artist drew was a mask, and this room holds the squarest of them.

The parts are a kit. An eye can be a red X in a box, a spiral in a box, a flower, a planet with a ring. A mouth can be white blocks, a row of fangs, a bar of grey teeth. Beads run along the edges, zigzags run along the brows, and a few tubes stand up on top like hair or a crown. Each mask is a new arrangement of the same parts, and the pleasure of the room is watching one mind play the same game twenty-four times without repeating a move. A mask is a face with the person taken out. These have the person's attention left in.

The summer had a shape outside the drawings too. In June the artist minted 300 nifties, the milestone piece, into the collector's wallet and dozens of others. In July the cats from the spring became a club of nearly ten thousand on Polygon. On August 11 the artist announced that the inks would be burned, every unsold copy, and the masks drawn after that date carry a price in the post that announced them: five of five, fifteen xDai. The last mask in the room, the long yellow face behind a cage of lines, was drawn on the last day of the mask summer, and it is the first in the room that looks like someone.

## Boxes Packed Tight

The first eight masks, June 8 to 25. Every one is filled edge to edge, and the X and the spiral trade places from eye to eye. On June 20 the artist marked three hundred inks on the board with a drawing minted free into the collector's wallet and many others; the masks went on the next day.

Below that, white boxes stand in a row like fence posts. Blue, pink, yellow and black fill every gap. It is drawn on June 8, 2022, four days after the first part of the series ends, and the whole room is already in it. Both eyes are cancelled. The mask summer begins with a face that has decided, before anyone looks at it, not to look back.

## Jaws and Crowns

Late June to mid July. The box becomes a head with a jaw, teeth get bigger, and crowns of spikes stand up along the top. These are the weeks of the chic city cat club, when the artist was sending a free cat to every holder of an ink.

**#157** (label) A purple ground, and in it a head with a yellow crown, two eyes that are each a mouth of fangs, and below them a wide grille of white teeth in a pink box. The face is drawn in black and white line over orange, blue and green scribble, and purple zigzag runs down the right side. It is the mask with the most teeth in the room: two sets in the eyes and one across the jaw. A crown over eyes that bite is the first crowned mask of the summer, from June 28. The crown comes back in September and October on nearly every head, and the thing it sits on never gets kinder than this.

## Machines and Mazes

July 19 to August 19. The boxes turn into machines and mazes, and three of the last four masks are among the most worked drawings in the room. From August 16 each one was posted with its price, fifteen xDai, and from August 24 the price fell to five.

With 334 recorded strokes it is one of the two most worked masks in the room, and the only one with a mustache. It was made on August 12, the day after the burn announcement, and it is the funniest mask of the summer. Whatever the burning meant to the artist, the next morning's drawing was a dandy.

**#220** (line) A mask with a crown of spikes and two spiral eyes in boxes, one yellow and one white, a row of ringed teeth below and bars of yellow and blue on both sides.

**#229** (label) A long yellow face with two pale eyes and a mouth of white and black bars, behind a cage of thin yellow and white lines that cross the whole square. Stripes of pink, red and green stand behind the face, and a crown of white and yellow zigzag sits on its brow. Where the first mask of June packed its boxes to the edges, this one stands back and lets the lines fall over it. It is the last work of the mask summer, drawn August 19, 2022, and posted that day at fifteen xDai with the rest of the week's masks. The eyes are not spirals or crosses. They are eyes, pale and level, and after ten weeks of masks the face behind the cage is looking out.

## Closing

In June, the first mask is packed to the edges with boxes, and its eyes are a red X and a purple cross. In August, the last is a long yellow face behind a cage of lines, with two pale eyes that are almost human. The parts never changed. The arrangement did, and so did the summer: the first mask was drawn in a month of giving things away, and the last in the week the artist began to burn.
