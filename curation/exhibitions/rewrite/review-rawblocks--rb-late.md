# Scribbled Heads (rawblocks--rb-late)

Spoken words: 1648, about 11.0 minutes.

## Introduction

Heads in a few lines, then heads in a storm of them. This room begins in November 2022 with faces drawn in loose colored line on bright grounds, yellow, orange, magenta, green, and ends in March 2023 with the collection piece. Between them the winter heads arrive: dark, scribbled, with teeth bared. The series went quiet for two months after November 23, and when it came back in January the heads had gone black.

The line heads are quick. A black loop for the skull, a few colored strokes for the features, the ground doing the rest. The scribbled heads are the opposite: hundreds of strokes piled on a dark face until only the eyes and the teeth show through. When the first of them appeared on January 18, 2023, Jamie Thingelstad bought it that night and wrote that he was excited to see nnxyz, one of his favorite artists, publishing again after a couple of months away, and had bought it to celebrate. It is the dark head with two mouths.

The ground goes dark. It is yellow on November 16. By January it is black, and by February the faces have no ground at all, only scribble. In February the artist began posting that unsold editions would burn in the next days, and in March nearly sixteen hundred copies were minted straight into a burn address. The collection piece was drawn on March 15, 2023, and that evening the collector bought it for 300 xDai and received the sixty-three Rawblocks within three hours. The two landscapes painted after it hang in the coda next door.

## Heads in a Few Lines

November 16 and 17, 2022. Four heads in loose line on bright grounds, each drawn in under a day of the last, in the week after the exchange went under. The ground is still bright.

**#42** (label) A head on yellow drawn in black line, with a red box around the face, one small eye at the left and one white ring at the right, and a grin of white teeth. Grey scribble fills the ground behind it, and a few blue lines run through the face. Sixty-two strokes. The head is a box inside a box, the way the masks were, but drawn in air: nothing is filled, and the yellow comes through everywhere. It was drawn on November 16, 2022, five days after the bankruptcy, and it is the lightest head in the wing, as if the artist had put the paint down and was drawing to see what was left. What was left was the box, the ring, the grin, and a lot of yellow.

**#43** (line) A head in profile on orange, drawn in black line with a blue X for an eye, a grin of white and black teeth, and an ear drawn as a spiral.

**#44** (line) A head on magenta in black and colored line, two ringed eyes, a crest of black scribble for hair, and a mouth of white teeth in a red box.

**#45** (line) A head in profile on green, drawn in a single pink and yellow line, with a red eye and a mouth of white teeth at the right.

## The Scribble Arrives

November 22 and 23, 2022. Three heads where the scribble comes in: a purple head with yellow hair, an orange cat head, and a head lit by a beam. Then two months of silence.

**#46** (line) A purple head with a crest of yellow strokes for hair, two ringed eyes, and a wide grin of white teeth, in colored scribble on purple.

**#47** (label) A cat head on orange, drawn in thick black loops over teal and yellow paint, with two eyes, one a spiral and one a white ring, and a small grin. The ears are two black points. A black shadow spreads under the head like spilled ink. The scribble here is black and heavy, and it covers the face so that the features are found inside it. The dark heads begin here, on November 23, 2022, and then the board went quiet for fifty-six days. A cat with a spiral eye was the first creature of the spring, drawn flat and humming on teal. This one is the same cat under a pile of black, and it is the last thing the artist drew before leaving for the winter.

**#48** (line) A head on purple with spiky hair, two boxed eyes, and a yellow beam pouring from its mouth to the right, drawn in colored scribble over dark paint.

## Winter Heads

January 18 to February 15, 2023. Eight dark heads in scribble, with teeth bared, and one yellow figure with bones. The first was the collector's celebration purchase; the February ones were drawn as the burning was announced.

**#49** (label) A dark head with two mouths of white teeth, one above the other, a red propeller for one eye and a yellow ring for the other, drawn in white, blue and red line over black. A black flower hangs at the upper right. The head is outlined in heavy white, and the two mouths fill the lower half. It is the first of the winter heads, drawn January 18, 2023, and the first with the teeth doubled. It was the first thing the artist posted after two months, and Jamie Thingelstad bought it that night for fifteen xDai, to celebrate, he wrote, that one of his favorite artists was publishing again. A head with two mouths is a head that has more to say than one mouth can hold. It came back from the winter talking twice.

**#50** (line) A dark head in yellow, blue and black scribble with a blue X over one eye, a box of teeth where the other should be, and a grin of yellow teeth below.

**#53** (line) A dark round head in scribble with one eye a red ring and the other a red oval, a grin of white teeth, and a white line running down the brow.

**#54** (label) A tall dark head on brown, two hollow eyes with spiky lids, each with a small ring inside, a long dark nose, and a wide mouth of white teeth in red. The head is drawn in grey and brown scribble with a heavy black edge. Two months after the head on yellow in sixty-two strokes, this is the same head drawn in darkness: a box of a face, two eyes, a grin, with the ground gone black. The eyes are hollow and the lids are spiked, and the grin is clenched: it is the face of someone holding on. It was drawn on January 23, 2023, the same day as the woman in yellow light next door. The artist drew the softest person in the series and the hardest head on the same day.

## March 14 and 15

The last night. Four works between March 14 and 15, 2023, and then the collection piece, drawn last, bought that evening, and followed within hours by the whole series.

**#60** (line) Three figures in a frame, a skull, a white skeleton and a pink robot, drawn in colored line over a crowded ground, with a small black figure at the lower right, the night before the last day.

**#61** (line) A mask with a bird's head for one eye, its beak open on white teeth, a red ringed eye beside it, and a beam of red crossing from the upper right, on blue.

**#62** (line) A figure with a sun for a head runs across red, in a blue shirt, one arm raised, drawn in red and white line over soft teal discs, running on the last day.

**#63** (line) A round mask split in two, half yellow and half brown, with a ringed eye on each side, a red target for a mouth, in a frame of grey, drawn March 15, 2023, an hour before the collection piece.

**#0000** (label) The collection piece, an edition of one, drawn last on March 15, 2023. A crowd of figures from the series in one square: a black crow at the top, a skull head on a striped body, a yellow sun-face with a grin, a black cat with X eyes, a blue skull in a ring, a small masked head. Every mark of the series is here, the beams, the X, the crown, the teeth. Where the first work of May 2022 packed a single mask to the frame, this one packs the whole series. That evening Jamie Thingelstad bought it for 300 xDai, the third origin piece he had bought in seven months, and in the hour around midnight UTC the artist sent the other sixty-three Rawblocks to his wallet, the one named for the board. In the two hours after drawing this, the artist painted two landscapes with no face in them, and they hang next door. Then the burning finished, and when the artist came back to the board months later the work had a new name and no series.

## Closing

Look again at the head on yellow from November, sixty-two strokes of black line with the ground showing through, and then at the collection piece, where the square is full. The room went from one line to a crowd in four months, through a winter in which the artist burned the year's unsold inks and the heads got darker every week. The last of them is every creature of the series at once, and the night it was drawn the series went to the collector who had bought its first work on the day the market broke.
