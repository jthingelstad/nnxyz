## The project

nnxyz drew on Nifty Ink for three years. The first ink was created on
October 26, 2021, and the last on December 5, 2024. In between the artist
created 595 drawings there, set a price on nearly all of them, and sold
editions of 281 of them to fifty wallets. This collection holds 548 of those
drawings, and every one the collector owns.

Nifty Ink was a free drawing board on the xDai chain, built by Austin
Griffith in 2020 as a demonstration of what a cheap chain could do. Anyone
could draw in the browser with a round brush, set a price, and mint
editions for a few cents. The board recorded every stroke in order, with
its width, color and transparency, which is why these drawings can be
watched being made. It did not record time.

### 2021: Faces

The first four inks arrived in a single day. By December the artist was
making fifty a month, nearly all of them faces: a head in a square, eyes,
teeth, a ground of black or white. The series was called nifty faces and
numbered from #1. A second address drew Nifty Faces #12 on November 30 and
sent it to the first the same day.

### 2022: On the Block, and everything else

New Nifty on the Block began on December 8, 2021 and ran to 303 drawings in
this collection by October 2022, the artist's busiest year: 435 inks, with
peaks of 66 in May and 54 in July. Rawblocks began in May 2022 with two
drawings, then waited five months while On the Block finished.

The year was also the year the artist stepped off the board. In March and
April, fifty-two Abstract paintings were minted on Polygon under a contract
the artist named "nnxyz abstract", and forty-six of them were given away in
April to named people: austingriffith.eth, scrimshaw.eth, jasperart.eth,
danielsheldon.eth, superphiz.eth and others who had bought, liked or made
work on the board. In August the artist deployed an "index collection" on
Ethereum and Optimism, describing it as "an open never-ending collection
published independently on multiple blockchains ... planned completely
openly", and minted "i was famous" on Ethereum in editions of three:
Duchamp's Fountain, four times over, in Pop colors. In November came "lord
of the inks", one image of about five hundred of the artist's own Nifty Ink
drawings laid out as a wall, and on Optimism three small series with new
names: "Heaven or Hell", "oink" and "opticals".

### 2023: Closing

Rawblocks resumed in October 2022 and ended on March 15, 2023 at #65. That
month the artist did something the board allowed but few used. Every edition
slot that had not sold was minted straight to a dead address, 1,560 of them
in March alone and 2,895 across the project. Nothing was destroyed; the
unsold copies were simply put where no one could ever buy them, so that
each numbered series was finished. In July the remaining Abstract copies
went the same way.

The series each have an origin work, #0000, which was held back and sold
last as the key to a complete set. Nifty Faces #0000 and Rawblocks #0000
went to this collection in 2022 and 2023; Abstract #0000 followed in May
2023, and with it the fifty-two Abstract paintings. A fifty-third Abstract
token, "#1", was minted and burned within three hours on March 30, 2022 and
never left the artist.

### Afterwards

The board did not go quiet. The artist went on liking other people's inks
through 2024, more than a hundred of them, and in December 2024 drew three
more. Two of them came to this collection in April 2025; the third went to
another buyer.

Off the board, the work kept its holders in mind. In April 2022 every owner
of a Nifty Face received its pixelated twin, "nifty faces px", on Polygon.
Later came a cat club "with no roadmap, no utility", "Friends Of Country Air",
"cringe-eez doodles", and three re-issued contracts whose copies were
airdropped to the people who already held the originals. On Tezos, from August 2022, came
"canvas everywhere", the "untitled" heads, "kanji", "i send you summer", GMGL
and ScreenSwindler, with the artist's bio there reading "born readymade to
please the eye, intentionally flawed to show the arbitrariness of
insignificance". This collection holds forty-six of those later works, shown
in their own room.

## Who was there

Fifty wallets bought from nnxyz on Nifty Ink. One collector, Jamie
Thingelstad, bought from three wallets (thingelstad.eth,
nnxyz.thingelstad.eth and niftyink.thingelstad.eth): 121 of the 283 sales,
and about half of the 4,026 xDai the drawings earned. His children's wallets
bought three inks each on May 10, 2022, the day the family started
collecting. The complete sets did not pass through the market; the artist
sent them directly, 508 drawings in three batches in August 2022, October
2022 and March 2023.

Austin Griffith, who built the board, funded the artist's first Ethereum
wallet with 0.06 ETH on February 1, 2022, bought eighteen inks, and received
gifts in 2023 and 2024. The others who bought most were iamjim.eth,
jaxcodes.eth, justn.eth and a handful of addresses without names.

The artist was a visitor as well as a maker. Over three years nnxyz liked
345 inks by 93 other artists on the board, most of all by jasperart.eth,
ekeko.eth, dashmdash.eth, danielsheldon.eth and chiliprawn.eth, three of
them by scrimshaw. Several of the same names received Abstract paintings.
The circle was small and the artist knew who was in it.

Nearly everything the drawings earned left the chain: about 3,969 xDai was
bridged out in sixty-seven transactions, most of it in 2022.

## In the artist's words

The artist's bios, where they exist, say the same thing three ways. On
Linktree: "born readymade to please the eye / flawed imperfect on purpose". On
objkt: "born readymade to please the eye, intentionally flawed to show the
arbitrariness of insignificance". On Instagram: "A drama of the arbitrariness
& insignificance of latent realities in the visible public space". On X the
bio is empty and the location reads "latent space".

On Nifty Ink, March 2, 2022: "Why I love Nifty Ink. Easy and fun entry into
the NFT space for artists. Tool works without problems. Easy upgrades of the
artwork into the mainchain ETH. No entry costs to mint the work on the Gnosis
chain. Nice and active community. Thanks @austingriffith." And on June 1,
2024: "The best feature of Nifty Ink is the playback of the doodles. If you
like Bob Ross, you can look at my over 500 doodles and see how each one was
painted."

On the gifts, April 12, 2022: "It was important to me to create some value
outside of Nifty Ink for all owners as a small thank you."

On the closing, April 8, 2022: "I'm extremely bullish about Nifty Ink and my
nifties. I won't stop until the site shuts down." August 11, 2022: "This is my
first NFT on Nifty Ink, which was also my first NFT sold. Therefore, my first
ink will be the last burned." August 23, 2022: "Just a little reminder that
all 400 created nifties on Nifty Ink will be gradually burned. Everything
after that is part of a new story."

On the index collection, August 2, 2022: "The name of the collection is also
fixed: nnxyz Index Collection. Speaking of unloved works being lost or
perished, I couldn't help but think of artist books, a grossly underrated
area. Among other things, of course, I had to think of Andy Warhol's Index
Book." And the day before: "If collectors or curators happen to be reading
this, collect and curate more art that you don't like, because the artist who
isn't what you're looking for today may have evolved into someone tomorrow who
opens a whole new world for you."

On the artist's life, in the description of "Heaven or Hell" (Tezos, August
16, 2022): "An artist's life can be heaven or hell. Depending on the
circumstances and the current contemporary culture, you can make it or die
trying." And in "shrine of - create, try, hope, die" (March 19, 2024): "insert
coin, create, try, hope, die, Game over, repeat."

On what art is, June 20, 2024: "There is no such thing as bad or good art.
There are only reasons for creating it, the meaning that a work may have as a
result, and the form it follows." July 30, 2025: "Art is an imprint of the
time and society in which an artist moves. Art is therefore always ethical and
political, even if an artist denies it." And on explaining, June 3, 2024: "It
is not the task of a work to immediately open itself fully to the recipient,
nor is it the task of the artist to explain his tools or his process."

Each quotation is a post by @0000nnxyz, saved by id and date in
`curation/research/social/x-posts.json`, with the thirty chosen for the
gallery and their screenshots in `quotes.json`. Light edits for print are
limited to expanding handles and removing emoji; nothing is paraphrased.

## How to read this page

A wallet is an address, not a person. Where an address has an Ethereum name
it is given; where it does not, it is counted. Amounts are in xDai, the
board's currency, worth about a dollar each. The ledgers are public; the
addresses and contracts are listed in the research record.

## Notes for the collector

- The page names you. Say if you want the paragraph about your purchases
  shorter, longer, or in your own words.
- It states the burns as deliberate closing. The contract has no "burn"
  event; this is the gallery's reading of 2,895 mints to a dead address.
- "Nifty Ink was a free drawing board ... built by Austin Griffith in 2020"
  needs one citation before publication (the Nifty Ink Medium post linked
  from nifty.ink, or BuidlGuidl's page). The Mac session can capture it.
- 595 is Nifty Ink's own count for the main address (read October 16, 2026);
  the ledger shows 592 successful createInk calls. The gallery's 548 are the
  owned subset, and the depth pass confirmed that no owned nnxyz ink is
  missing from the archive.
- The artist posts as a European (two posts refer to "DE" and "as a
  European"). That is a sourced fact and is left off the page unless you want
  it; the page says nothing else about the person.
