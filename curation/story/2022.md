Most of this collection was drawn in one year. Of the 548 nnxyz drawings here, 425 are dated 2022, and the artist's ledger shows 435 inks created on the board that year, out of 595 in three. It was also the year the collector arrived, the year the artist began publishing on other chains, and the year the crypto market fell twice. This page lays them beside each other, month by month. The prose is the gallery's; the block after each month is the record, with the rooms where that month's drawings hang.

## January

The year opens quietly. Twenty-nine inks, seventeen of them Faces and eleven of New Nifty on the Block, which had begun on December 8 and was past #20. On January 28 the artist posted "100 nifties," the drawing for one hundred inks on the board, and "dropped this piece into the pockets of some niftians." The collector had not yet found the board.

{{month 2022-01}}

## February

The artist's first Ethereum wallet is funded on February 1 by austingriffith.eth, who built the board. Forty-seven inks, the busiest month of the project so far, and the best sales month until May: nineteen payments, 268 xDai, most of it from a few regulars. The faces are still the main work; the crowds and silhouettes of On the Block are beginning.

{{month 2022-02}}

## March

On March 2 the artist writes out why Nifty Ink: "Easy and fun entry into the NFT space for artists. Tool works without problems. No entry costs." On March 20 The Weekly Thing recommends the board to its readers, found through its Gitcoin grant: "not about going to the moon." On March 30 the first Abstract paintings are minted on Polygon. The same month, fifty-one unsold editions are minted to the dead address, the first sign of the closing that would take over the autumn.

{{month 2022-03}}

## April

The month everything starts. The artist's busiest month on X, 311 posts, most of them the Abstract gifts and the pixel twins: forty-six Abstract paintings go to the board's regulars from April 2, and from April 27 every holder of a Nifty Face receives its "px" twin. The collector buys Nifty Faces #109 on April 14, draws UKRAINE on April 23, and on April 25 receives "200 nifties" minted straight into his wallet. Thirty-one inks drawn.

{{month 2022-04}}

## May

Sixty-six inks, the most of any month, and seventy-eight sales for 681 xDai, more than any month before or after. On May 6 the artist notices "552 NFTs as nnxyz." On May 9 Terra's stablecoin loses its peg and by May 13 the crypto market has fallen with it; the drawings of that week are the beams, the glossy heads and the summer figures the collector bought on May 10 for a single xDai each. On May 11 he writes, "I'm the top collector on Nifty Ink." On May 23 the artist mints New Nifty on the Block #100 into his wallet. Rawblocks #1 and #2 are drawn on May 8 and 9, then wait.

{{month 2022-05}}

## June

Thirty-eight inks and the start of the mask summer. Sales fall to eight. On June 20 the artist mints "300 nifties" into the collector's wallet, the third milestone piece to go to him as it happened.

{{month 2022-06}}

## July

Fifty-four inks, the second-busiest month, and one sale, for one xDai. The artist is working on Polygon: on July 15 the Chic City Cat Club sends a cat to every holder of an ink, "no roadmap, no utility," and on July 20 the artist decides to burn the Canvas is Everywhere series and move that kind of work to Tezos. The same day, Nifty Faces #0000 is drawn: the origin work, held back as "the last and only 1/1 from this series."

{{month 2022-07}}

## August

The turn. On August 2 the index collection is named, after Warhol's Index Book, and described as "openly planned, never-ending." On August 5 its contract is deployed on Ethereum, with Heaven or Hell following on three chains. On August 11: "my first ink will be the last burned." On August 13 the collector buys Faces #0000 for 500 xDai, and within hours the artist sends him 126 Faces and the first five of On the Block. On August 21 "i was famous" is minted on Ethereum, and on August 23 the artist writes that all four hundred nifties will be gradually burned and "everything after that is part of a new story." Fifty-three inks drawn, 170 unsold editions sent to the dead address, twenty-six sales for 691 xDai, most of it the one.

{{month 2022-08}}

## September

Thirty inks and two sales. Ethereum completes the Merge on September 15. The burn gathers pace: 213 editions to the dead address. On September 28 the collector draws "nnxyz fan art #0001," his block letters spelling the artist's name. The artist's posts thin out; after this month there are two more in the year.

{{month 2022-09}}

## October

Thirteen inks, the fewest of the year, and the end of New Nifty on the Block on October 15. The next morning the collector buys its origin work, #0000, for 750 xDai, and that afternoon 275 drawings arrive in his wallet over four hours. Two hundred and eighty-five editions go to the dead address. The artist likes fifty inks by other artists, more than in any other month: a visitor again, as the series close. Rawblocks resumes on October 30.

{{month 2022-10}}

## November

Forty-three inks, nearly all Rawblocks: the portraits of the first ten days are drawn as FTX fails, from November 6 to its bankruptcy on November 11. The same week the artist mints oink on Optimism, eleven painted pictures, and the collector buys #1, a lake, on November 7. opticals follow from November 16, and on November 20 "lord of the inks," one picture of five hundred of the artist's own inks, on Ethereum. On Thanksgiving morning the collector moves an nnxyz ink from Gnosis to Ethereum over brunch to show two friends how a bridge works. Three hundred and ten editions go to the dead address.

{{month 2022-11}}

## December

Nothing. No inks, no posts, no sales. The artist's next ink is in January 2023, and the collector's note on it says why it mattered: "I was excited to see nnxyz, one of my favorite artists, publishing again."

{{month 2022-12}}

## What the year shows

Read as a line, 2022 is a drawing board filling up and then being closed on purpose. The spring is the most generous stretch of the project, gifts and milestones and the busiest drawing month, and it ends the week the market falls. The summer is masks. The autumn is the burn, the handovers and the move to other chains, with the last series drawn against the second crash. The rooms were hung by what the drawings look like, not by the calendar, but every room page gives its dates, and the [map](/map/) walks them in order.
