The artist has never given an interview that the research found, and has said in public that explaining is not the artist's job: "it is not the task of a work to immediately open itself fully to the recipient, nor is it the task of the artist to explain his tools or his process." What exists instead is a public account on X with more than two and a half thousand posts, most of them announcing a drawing with its price, and a handful of bio lines on the sites where the work is sold. The thirty posts below are the ones that say something about the work, the board, the burn, the later collections or what art is for. They are printed verbatim, with three light edits: links and hashtag-only lines are dropped, handles are written out as names, and emoji are removed. Nothing is paraphrased and nothing is added. The line under each post is the gallery's, saying what it bears on.

Each is dated and linked to the original, so it can be read as posted. The posts that announce a single drawing are not here; where one exists for a drawing in the collection, it is printed on that drawing's page, with the price the artist asked. There are 263 of those.

The bios are short and say the same thing in three ways: made to please the eye, flawed on purpose, about the arbitrariness of insignificance. They are the nearest thing to a statement, and the rooms take them as one.
